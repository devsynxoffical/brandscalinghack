When should an ecommerce brand hire a TikTok ads agency?

Hire a TikTok ads agency once Meta is stable and you can supply native creator video every week. TikTok rewards volume and authenticity more than polish, so the agency's job is mostly creative: briefing creators, testing hooks and turning organic winners into Spark Ads that sell to your Shopify store.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a TikTok ads agency do for an ecommerce brand?

A TikTok ads agency produces native creator videos, runs in-feed and Spark Ads campaigns, and manages TikTok Shop and affiliate creators where the product fits. Brand Scaling runs TikTok alongside Meta from one creative engine, so winning hooks are recut for both platforms and measured on blended MER.

Do you also run TikTok Shop?

Yes, as a separate service from TikTok ads. We set up or audit the shop listing, recruit and brief affiliate creators, structure commissions and sample seeding, and run GMV Max and Spark Ads on the best affiliate videos, all tied to the same offer and margin model as your Shopify store. The details are on our TikTok Shop scaling services page.

Are TikTok ads better than Meta ads for ecommerce?

Neither is better in general. Meta usually scales more predictably for most DTC brands; TikTok can win for visual, demo-friendly and impulse products with a younger audience. We test TikTok once creative supply and tracking are solid, and keep the budget on whichever channel delivers the lower new-customer CAC.

How much should I spend on TikTok ads to start?

Plan on enough budget to exit learning on one campaign: roughly 20 to 50 times your target cost per purchase over the first two weeks, plus a creative budget for 8 to 12 new videos a week. Brand Scaling partners typically spend $10,000 to $20,000+ a month across paid social.

What are Spark Ads?

Spark Ads run an existing organic TikTok post, from your account or a creator's with permission, as a paid ad. The post keeps its likes, comments and creator handle, which makes it look native and usually lowers cost per click compared with a standard in-feed ad.

Do I need TikTok Shop to advertise on TikTok?

No. Many Shopify brands advertise on TikTok and send traffic to their own store. TikTok Shop adds in-app checkout and affiliate creators, which helps impulse products convert, but it is a separate decision we make based on margin, fulfilment and category.

TikTok ads or TikTok Shop: which one you need

This page is about TikTok advertising: in-feed and Spark Ads that send people to your own store. TikTok Shop is a different model. The sale happens inside the app, affiliate creators earn a commission on each order, and the work is listings, samples and creator management as much as ads. It now has its own page.

The two can run together, but the decision is separate. TikTok ads suit brands that want customers and data on their own Shopify store, with email and SMS follow-up afterwards. TikTok Shop suits impulse-friendly products with healthy margins after commission, and brands willing to manage fulfilment and creator relationships inside TikTok.

How a TikTok brief differs from a Meta brief

A Meta ad can open on a product shot. On TikTok an ad that looks like an ad is skipped, so we brief creators on the first two seconds, give them talking points instead of a script, and let them film in their own setting.

  • Open on a person, a reaction or a problem, not a logo or a packshot.
  • Use native text overlays and the creator's own voice rather than polished voiceover.
  • Show the product in use within the first few seconds.
  • Ask for three or four hook variations from every creator.
  • Get Spark Ads authorisation codes at the brief stage, not after a post performs.

How to judge TikTok results fairly

TikTok buyers often see an ad, leave, and buy later through search, Instagram or a direct visit, so TikTok's own dashboard and Shopify rarely agree. We judge the channel on three numbers together: the platform-reported cost per purchase, new-customer CAC in Shopify for the period, and blended MER before and after TikTok spend started. If MER holds or improves as TikTok spend grows, the channel is adding customers, even when its in-platform ROAS looks lower than Meta's.
