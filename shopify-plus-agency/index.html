What does a Shopify Plus agency do?

A Shopify Plus agency helps high-volume brands get more from Shopify's enterprise plan. A build-focused agency handles themes, apps and migrations. A growth-focused one, like Brand Scaling, runs the ads and creative that bring buyers and uses Plus features such as checkout customization to convert more of them.

When a brand needs a Shopify Plus agency

Shopify Plus is Shopify's plan for high-volume merchants. Moving to Plus does not grow revenue by itself; it gives more control over checkout, automation and selling in more markets. The brands that get value from a Plus agency are the ones whose growth is limited by conversion and acquisition, not by the platform.

What we run for Shopify Plus brands

The same growth system we run for every partner, adjusted for the scale and the extra control Plus gives:

Paid acquisition at scale

Meta as the main engine on broad and Advantage+ campaigns, TikTok for creator-led discovery and Google Shopping for search demand. Budgets rise about 20% at a time while blended MER and new-customer CAC hold.

Creative volume

30 to 50+ new ad creatives a month, briefed from a 45+ cell hook and angle matrix, so the account never depends on a few tired ads.

Checkout and offer CRO

Plus lets brands customize checkout. We use that for trust signals, offers and upsells that lift order value, and test each change on revenue per session.

Retention

Klaviyo email and SMS flows built on Shopify data, so the second order does not depend on more ad spend.

How Shopify Plus agency pricing works

Shopify charges for the Plus plan itself; that is separate from any agency. Our fee is a monthly amount quoted after the free Brand Growth Audit, sometimes combined with a share of ad spend, and your ad spend is paid directly to the platforms. Partners typically spend at least $10,000 to $20,000 a month on ads.

How to choose a Shopify Plus agency

Decide first whether you need a build or growth. For growth, ask for case studies with ad spend shown, who makes the creative, how profit is reported and what happens in the first 30 days.

  • Verified results from real ad accounts, with spend visible
  • In-house creative production and a weekly testing cadence
  • Reporting on MER, new-customer CAC and contribution margin, not only ROAS
  • A clear split of work with your developer for custom builds

Frequently asked questions

What is a Shopify Plus agency?

An agency that works with brands on Shopify Plus, Shopify's plan for high-volume merchants. Some focus on builds and migrations; others, like Brand Scaling, focus on growth through ads, creative and conversion work.

Do you build Shopify Plus stores?

We improve product pages, landing pages, offers and the parts of checkout Plus lets you customize. For full theme builds, custom apps or migrations we work alongside your developer.

Do I need Shopify Plus to work with you?

No. Most partners are on standard Shopify plans. Plus adds checkout customization and more automation, which helps at higher volume, but the growth system is the same.

How much does a Shopify Plus agency cost?

Agency fees are separate from Shopify's Plus plan fee. We quote a monthly fee after the free audit; partners typically spend at least $10,000 to $20,000 a month on ads.

Do you do CRO for Shopify Plus stores?

Yes. Most of our CRO work is on Shopify and Shopify Plus, including checkout, offers, bundles and post-purchase upsells, measured on revenue per session.
