## 2. The biggest problems, ranked by business impact

### Problem 1: Google does not trust or prioritise the site yet

**Evidence:**
- **No external links.** Bing lists 0 linking websites.
- **Little crawling.** Google has crawled 56 of 160 sitemap URLs. It crawled no new URL between 16 and 18 Nov.
- **Commercial searches rank far back.** Agency and service searches average **position 66** (53 queries, 191 impressions, 0 clicks).
- **No brand demand.** Branded searches were 4 impressions in 30 days.

**What competitors have that we lack.** Live results for "dtc shopify agency", "tiktok shop scaling services" and "meta ads agency for ecommerce brands", checked 19 Nov:
- **"Best agencies" lists**, mostly published by agencies themselves (insidea, stryde, 99minds, flighted, knowlify).
- **Agency pages with third-party proof:**
  - Partner badges: Meta Agency Partner, TikTok Shop Partner, Shopify Plus partner tiers.
  - Clutch, GoodFirms and Upwork profiles.
  - Published price ranges. Top Growth Marketing, for example, shows $2,000 to $10,000 a month.
- **Press and syndication.** For example, a barchart.com story about a Shopify agency.

We have none of these. Content quality cannot make up for that on commercial searches.

### Problem 2: We targeted the wrong keywords first

The searches the site was shown for in 30 days, grouped by intent:

| Intent | Queries | Impressions | Clicks | Average position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Informational (for example "roas formula", "what is roas", "meta andromeda") | 96 | 328 | 0 | 76 |
| Commercial: agency or service | 53 | 191 | 0 | 66 |
| Calculators and tools | 21 | 80 | 0 | 51 |
| AI-style long prompts | 14 | 25 | 0 | 32 |
| Brand | 4 | 4 | 0 | 23 |

**Honest verdict:**
- **Early content went after broad definitions** ("what is ROAS", "ROAS formula", "Meta Andromeda").
  - Shopify, Triple Whale and other high-authority sites own those results.
  - People who search them are learning, not hiring an agency.
  - They bring 37% of our impressions, at position 76 to 92, with no realistic route to page 1 soon. **This was the wrong first battle for a new domain.**
- **Our "page-1 rankings" are mostly AI-style long questions.** Each has 1 to 4 impressions. One of them is a full persona prompt ("about me: fast-growing dtc brand founder…"), most likely generated by an AI tool or tracker rather than typed by a buyer **[Assumption]**. They are not a business result, and earlier reports gave them too much weight.

### Problem 3: We published too many pages, too fast, for a site Google barely crawls

On 16 Oct the site had 7 page templates. We added **111 pages in about 4 weeks**. Their results after 30 days:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core, service and hub pages | 30 | 20 | 19 | 637 |
| Blog | 30 | 7 | 5 | 351 |
| Case studies | 43 | 7 | 4 | 36 (+40 on an old redirected URL) |
| Tools | 10 | 4 | 3 | 29 |
| Locations | 7 | 7 | 4 | 15 |
| Comparisons | 4 | 3 | 2 | 13 |
| Industries | 15 | 5 | 4 | 10 |
| Case niche hubs | 5 | 2 | 2 | 8 |
| Benchmark | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **Glossary** | **15** | **1** | **0** | **0** |

**Honest verdict:**
- The pages are not thin or duplicated. Each is 1,200 to 1,800 words, and the overlap score between pages is 0.02 to 0.06.
- But for a new domain with no links, publishing 111 pages did not create visibility. Google crawled about 25 of them.
- **Glossary (15 pages), industries (15) and the benchmark page earned 10 impressions combined.** With today's crawl rate they were not a good use of effort.
- The **blog** earns impressions (351), but almost all at positions 77 to 92, for informational searches (Problem 2).

/blog/how-much-does-a-dtc-agency-cost is "discovered, not indexed".

**Earlier mistake:** the Google Indexing API was used to submit these pages repeatedly, and that was reported as progress. Google documents that API only for job and livestream pages. 48 of 52 URLs submitted 3 or more times were still uncrawled (16 Nov analysis).

### Problem 5: Lead tracking was broken, so conversion could not be judged

These issues were verified in a browser on 19 Nov:
1. **42 "Book" buttons in 29 files recorded nothing in GA4.** These include the home hero's "BOOK YOUR BRAND GROWTH AUDIT" and "Book A Discovery Call". The click tracker only listened to links, and these are buttons.
2. **The booking itself is invisible to GA4.** It happens inside an embedded Google Calendar page.
3. **GA4 was counting development visits.** Pages from `localhost` appear in the GA4 page report.
4. **`generate_lead` is not marked as a key event in GA4**, which is why "key events" shows 0.

**What we can actually say about conversion:**
- About 3 real prospects arrived from Google search in 30 days. That is too few to measure a conversion rate.
- The honest answer to "why don't organic visitors convert" is: **there are almost no organic visitors yet, and the tracking could not show what the few did.**

### Problem 6: Our own FAQ additions compete with our dedicated pages

| Search | Pages Google shows (impressions, position) | Dedicated page |
|---|---|---|
| tiktok shop scaling services | /growth (26, 70.4), / (11, 67.5) | /tiktok-shop-agency (1, 64) |
| dtc ecommerce cro agency | / (8, 80.6), /shopify-agency (7, 72.3) | /cro-agency (1 impression in total, 91) |

**Cause:**
- On 10 and 12 Nov we added exact-match FAQ questions to /growth, /tiktok-ads-agency and /shopify-agency: "Do you offer TikTok Shop scaling services?" and "Do you work as a DTC ecommerce CRO agency?".
- Those pages were already crawled; the dedicated pages had each been crawled only once.

This is real, but small: every page involved sits at position 64 to 91.

### Problem 7: Claims that are hard to verify

- The site says "150+ case studies", but it publishes **126**.
- It says "$50M+ ad spend managed", with no public evidence.

Inconsistent numbers weaken trust for buyers and for Google's quality systems **[Assumption about the ranking effect]**. Only the owner can confirm or correct these.

### Not a cause (verified)

- Titles, descriptions and H1s: 0 issues across 243 pages.
- Duplicate content.
- Rendering: 13 crawlers receive identical HTML.
- Sitemaps: read daily, 0 errors.
- Robots and canonicals.
- Redirects.
- Speed affects the home page, not rankings at position 60 or worse. Home was 25/100 and is now 54/100 (lab test).

## 5. Executed today

The full record, with reasons, URLs, validation methods and before/after results, is in `docs/seo-change-log.md`.

1. **Booking buttons now send `book_click` and `generate_lead`.**
   - These are the 42 buttons across 29 files that used to record nothing.
   - Validated in a browser on desktop and iPhone: before, no event; after, both events.
2. **GA4 now loads only on www.brandscalinghacks.com**, so development traffic is no longer recorded.
   - Validated: gtag.js is requested on the live domain, and not on localhost.
3. **Exact-match FAQ questions that competed with /tiktok-shop-agency and /cro-agency were reworded** on /growth, /tiktok-ads-agency and /shopify-agency.
   - The answers now point to the dedicated pages.
   - Validated: build, SEO audit and a desktop and iPhone browser check passed.
   - The ranking effect is measured from Search Console in 2 to 4 weeks.

Nothing else was changed.
