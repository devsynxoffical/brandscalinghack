  - Unique titles and descriptions on every page: 0 duplicates and 0 too long, last checked 18 Nov.
  - One H1 per page.
  - Alt text on every image.
- **Tracking and indexing tools:**
  - GA4 installed with lead events: WhatsApp, booking, email and phone clicks, scroll depth and calculator use.
  - Bing Webmaster and IndexNow connected.
- **Speed:**
  - Home page mobile score went from **25 to 54** (Lighthouse, 18 Nov). The phone hero video dropped from 8.5 MB to 2.6 MB, and the main thread is blocked for about 0.6 s instead of 3.8 s.
  - Case page loading improved: hub to case went from 0.98 s to 0.45 s, and the blank screen is gone.
- **Monitoring:** keyword and page tracking runs every 12 hours (`docs/seo-tracking.md`).

**Overall:** 471 files changed across the month.

## 5. Honest assessment

**What is working:**
- Google now shows the site 6 to 7 times more often each week than at the start.
- The average position improved from about 65 to 38 within the month.
- Page-1 rankings appear for AI-style questions and for some calculator keywords.
- Bing indexes 92 pages.

**What is not working yet:**
1. **Clicks.** Only 1 click came from a real US prospect, because 71% of keywords rank beyond page 5.
2. **Crawling.** Google has crawled only 56 of 160 pages, so about 100 new pages cannot rank yet.
3. **Links.** No other website links to the site. This is the main reason Google crawls slowly and ranks low.
4. **Leads.** GA4 recorded 0 key events. `generate_lead` still needs to be marked as a key event in GA4.
5. **Test traffic in GA4.** GA4 also records visits from localhost (development testing). These should be filtered out in GA4.
