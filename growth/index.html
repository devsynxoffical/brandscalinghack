How do you choose an ecommerce growth agency?

Choose an ecommerce growth agency that can show real results in your price range and niche, produces creative in-house, and reports on blended MER and contribution margin rather than platform ROAS alone. Ask who owns your ad accounts and store, how many new creatives you get each month, and what happens in the first 90 days.

What an Ecommerce Growth Agency Does

A growth agency is measured on business outcomes — revenue, cost per acquisition (CAC), and net contribution margin. Traditional agencies manage dashboards in isolation; we align creative volume, media buying, and Shopify checkout speed into a single feedback loop.

The Four Phases of Our Growth Engine

  1. Phase 1 — Foundation: Offer setup, sub-1s mobile Shopify, unit margin economics.
  2. Phase 2 — Test: Weekly UGC ad cadence, 45+ hook and angle matrix, Advantage+ audience testing.
  3. Phase 3 — Optimize: Conversion rate, ROAS and margin health, checkout friction removal.
  4. Phase 4 — Scale: High-volume creative lab (40+ assets a month), global market expansion.

How is a growth agency different from a performance marketing agency?

A performance marketing agency focuses on paid media results. A growth agency owns the full loop around the media: creative volume, store conversion rate, average order value and retention. Brand Scaling does both, which is why the engagement starts with a Brand Growth Audit rather than an ad account handover.

Which agency creates Instagram Reels and TikTok videos for brands, and what does it cost?

Brand Scaling scripts, sources creators for and edits short vertical videos (UGC, founder-led and product demos) for Instagram Reels, TikTok and Meta ads, and runs them in the ad accounts it manages, so each video is judged on CPA and ROAS rather than views. Creative is priced as part of the growth engagement and quoted after the Brand Growth Audit; partners typically spend at least $10,000 to $20,000 a month on ads.

What results should a $50K-a-month brand expect in 90 days?

In the first 14 days we fix tracking, offer and the product page; by day 45 most accounts have 2 to 3 proven creative concepts; by day 90 the target is a stable scaling budget at or above break-even ROAS with new-customer CAC trending down. Published case studies include $146,184 in 27 days at 3.64x ROAS and $2.3M in sales at 11.6x ROAS.

How ecommerce growth agencies are priced

Growth agencies usually charge a monthly retainer, a percentage of ad spend, a performance fee, or a combination. Because a growth engagement covers more than media buying, check what the fee includes: creative production, Shopify development and retention work are sometimes billed on top. Brand Scaling quotes after the Brand Growth Audit, once the scope is clear, and does not publish fixed prices because two brands at the same revenue can need very different work.
