From founder-run ads to a growth system

Many brands reach us after the founder has run the ads alone for the first year. The account works, but it has too many small campaigns, tracking that does not match Shopify, and creative that depends on whoever had time that week. We consolidate the account, fix tracking against real orders, and set up a weekly creative cadence from a 45+ cell hook and angle matrix so new winners keep arriving.

The goal for the first 90 days is simple: a stable CPA at a higher daily budget, measured on blended MER and contribution margin, not just platform ROAS.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have an office in Austin?

No. Our only office is in New York. We serve Austin and Central Texas brands remotely, one hour ahead of Central time.

Are you a Shopify agency for Austin brands?

Yes, for growth: we improve product pages, offers and checkout for paid traffic and run the ads that bring the traffic. For custom app development we work with your developer.

Do you run Facebook ads for Austin ecommerce brands?

Yes. Meta is usually the main channel, run on broad and Advantage+ campaigns with weekly creative testing and tracking checked against Shopify orders.

What size of Austin brand do you work with?

DTC brands with proven demand, doing about $20K to $2M+ a month and spending at least $10,000 to $20,000 a month on ads.

Can you take over ads the founder has been running?

Yes. We keep what already works, consolidate the account into fewer campaigns, fix tracking against Shopify orders and add a weekly creative cadence, so results do not dip during the handover.

How much does a Facebook ads agency in Austin cost?

Usually a monthly retainer, a share of ad spend or a mix, plus your ad spend. We quote after the free Brand Growth Audit.
