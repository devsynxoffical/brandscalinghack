Is Brand Scaling an ecommerce marketing agency based in New York?

Yes. Brand Scaling is an ecommerce marketing agency headquartered at 27 W 24th Street, Suite 702, New York, NY 10010. We run Meta, TikTok and Google ads, creative, Shopify CRO and Klaviyo retention for DTC brands, and New York founders can book an in-person Brand Growth Audit at the office by appointment.

An in-person audit at the office

Every engagement starts with a Brand Growth Audit: a 1-on-1 review with Gaurav of your ad account, creative, store and unit economics. Most audits happen on a video call, but if you are in New York you can ask to do it in person at the office. In-person audits are arranged by appointment, so book first through the contact page and we will confirm a time.

Some founders prefer this because it is easier to open the ad account, the Shopify admin and the margin spreadsheet on one table and work through them together. You leave with a written list of what to fix first, whether or not we work together after.

  • Ad account review: structure, spend split, creative fatigue and what is actually driving purchases
  • Creative review: which angles and hooks are carrying the account and which are missing
  • Store review: product page, offer, checkout and post-purchase flow
  • Unit economics: contribution margin per order and the break-even ROAS your prices allow

Working on Eastern time

Because the team works on Eastern time, a New York brand gets the same business hours we do. Weekly reporting calls sit in the normal working day, creative feedback can go back and forth within hours, and launch days for drops or sales get watched live rather than reviewed the next morning.

That matters most in Q4. During Black Friday and Cyber Monday we adjust budgets and swap creative through the day, and a brand on the same clock can approve offer changes in real time instead of waiting for a reply from another time zone.

What we run for New York brands

New York is commonly associated with fashion, beauty, jewelry and lifestyle brands, and those categories reward the same thing: a steady supply of new creative and a store that converts paid traffic. We run the full growth system rather than one channel in isolation.

  • Meta ads as the main acquisition engine, with Conversions API tracking set up properly
  • TikTok ads, Spark Ads and TikTok Shop for creator-led discovery
  • Google Shopping and Performance Max for people already searching for the product
  • Shopify CRO: product pages, bundles, post-purchase upsells and checkout
  • Klaviyo email and SMS flows so the second order does not depend on more ad spend
  • 30 to 50+ new ad creatives a month built from a 45+ cell hook and angle matrix

The first 90 days

Foundation runs from day 1 to 14: tracking, offer, margins and the product page. Test runs from day 15 to 45 with weekly creative from the hook and angle matrix. Optimize runs from day 45 to 90 and works on conversion rate, AOV and ROAS. Scale starts after day 90, raising budgets in 20 to 30% steps with cost caps and 40+ assets a month.

Sales tax is worth checking during Foundation. US sales tax is set by states and localities, and once a brand ships enough into other states it can owe tax there too. We do not give tax advice, but we make sure your margin model uses the price the customer actually pays.

Pricing

We quote after the audit. Agencies usually charge a monthly retainer, a percentage of ad spend, a performance fee or a mix, and the right model depends on your spend and margin. Being local does not change the price; it only changes where the first meeting can happen.
