Where is Brand Scaling based, and which markets do you serve?

Brand Scaling has one office, in New York at 27 W 24th Street, Suite 702. We run ads, creative and Shopify growth remotely for DTC brands across the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. Each page below explains how we work with brands in that market, including time zones, currency, tax and privacy rules.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only work with brands in these cities?

No. These pages cover the markets we are asked about most. We work with DTC brands anywhere in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia, all managed from our New York office. What matters more than the city is your margin, your offer and whether you are ready to spend at least $10K to $20K a month on ads.

Can I meet the team in person?

Yes, in New York. Brand Growth Audits can be held at our office at 27 W 24th Street, Suite 702 by appointment. For every other market the audit and the ongoing work run over video calls, shared dashboards and written weekly reports, which is how most of our client relationships work anyway.

How do you handle time zones?

We schedule a fixed weekly call in a slot that suits both sides and send written reports so nothing waits on a meeting. New York, Toronto and Miami share Eastern time, London overlaps with New York mornings, the US West Coast is three hours behind, and Sydney gets a report waiting each morning plus an early or late call.
