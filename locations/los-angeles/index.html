Creator and UGC-heavy categories

Brands in creator-led categories usually have plenty of content and not enough system. Posts that did well organically get boosted at random, creators are paid for one video with no plan for the next, and nobody knows which hook actually sold. The fix is to treat creator content as raw material for a testing machine.

We brief creators against a 45+ cell hook and angle matrix (five angles, three hook styles, three formats), so every video tests a specific idea. Partner brands get 30 to 50+ new creatives a month, including UGC produced through our sister studio SevenFigureStudio when a brand needs more volume than its own creators supply.

  • Hooks briefed one per video, so results show which opening worked
  • Raw phone footage edited into several cuts instead of one polished ad
  • Usage rights agreed in writing before paid use, including length of use
  • Winners moved from TikTok to Meta and back, rather than living on one platform

TikTok, Spark Ads and TikTok Shop

For creator-heavy brands TikTok is usually where new angles are found fastest. Spark Ads let you put paid budget behind an existing organic post, keeping its likes and comments, which often reads as more native than a fresh ad. TikTok Shop adds in-app checkout and a creator affiliate program, where creators earn commission on the sales they drive.

On Meta, partnership ads do a similar job by running ads through a creator's handle with their permission. We run both, and we keep the measurement honest: TikTok often starts demand that converts later on Meta, Google or email, so we look at blended results as well as in-platform numbers.

Disclosure, CCPA and CPRA

Two rules matter more than usual for creator-led brands. First, the FTC's Endorsement Guides apply nationwide and require creators to disclose a material connection, such as payment or free product. Branded content tools and clear on-screen disclosure handle most of this.

Second, as a California business you operate under CCPA as amended by CPRA, which lets consumers opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information, including data used for cross-context behavioral advertising. Pixels, Conversions API events and affiliate tracking all send data out, so we map them during Foundation for your counsel to review.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have an office in Los Angeles?

No. Our only office is in New York, at 27 W 24th Street, Suite 702. We serve Los Angeles brands remotely, with calls in Pacific working hours and creative shared through online folders. We do not have a local team, run local shoots or hold meetups in LA, and we prefer to be clear about that before you book.

Can you work with the creators we already use?

Yes. If you have creators or an affiliate roster, we brief them against the hook and angle matrix so each video tests a clear idea, then cut their footage into multiple ad versions. Where you need more volume, our sister studio SevenFigureStudio produces UGC. Usage rights for paid ads should be agreed with each creator in writing.

Is TikTok or Meta better for a Los Angeles creator-led brand?

Usually both, in different roles. TikTok is fast for finding new angles and works well with Spark Ads and TikTok Shop affiliates. Meta tends to carry more of the profitable scale once an angle is proven. We test on both and judge them on blended results, because TikTok often creates demand that is captured on other channels later.

Do creators have to disclose paid partnerships in ads?

Yes. The FTC's Endorsement Guides require creators to disclose a material connection to the brand, such as payment, commission or free product. On TikTok and Meta that usually means using the platform's branded content or paid partnership label and clear wording in the video or caption. We build disclosure into briefs rather than fixing it after launch.

How does CCPA affect tracking for an LA ecommerce brand?

CCPA, as amended by CPRA, gives California consumers the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, including for cross-context behavioral advertising. Pixels, Conversions API events and affiliate links can all involve sharing. We document what each one sends so your counsel can confirm the consent and opt-out setup you need.
