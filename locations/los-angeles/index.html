Yes. Brand Scaling has no Los Angeles office; we serve LA brands remotely from New York with calls scheduled in Pacific time. We run Meta and TikTok ads, creator-led creative, Google Shopping and Shopify CRO for DTC brands doing about $20K to $2M+ a month.

Creator-led ads for LA brands

Many LA founders already have creators, friends of the brand or a founder face that sells on camera. We turn that into a system: every product gets a hook and angle matrix of 45+ cells, creators film to briefs built from real customer language, and we test 30 to 50+ new ads a month so winners are ready before the current ones fatigue.

The same footage then feeds TikTok Spark Ads, Meta ads and the product page, so one shoot pays three times.

Services for Los Angeles DTC brands

Most LA partners use a mix of these, set up around the category and margin:

  • Meta and Instagram ads with weekly creative testing
  • TikTok ads, Spark Ads and TikTok Shop for beauty, fashion and wellness products
  • Shopify store work: product pages, bundles and checkout built for paid traffic
  • Google Shopping for people already searching for the product
  • Klaviyo email and SMS flows that turn first orders into repeat buyers

From first call to scaling, step by step

  1. Brand Growth Audit call (Week 0): A 1-on-1 review of your ad account, creative, store and unit economics with Gaurav Kapoor. Free, and you keep the findings.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have an office in Los Angeles?

No. Our only office is in New York. We serve Los Angeles brands remotely, with calls scheduled in Pacific time and a shared reporting dashboard.

Are you a Shopify developer in Los Angeles?

We are a growth agency, not a theme development shop. We rebuild product pages, landing pages and offers for conversion, and work with your developer when custom app work is needed.

Can you use our own creators and founder content?

Yes. We brief your creators or our network with a hook and angle matrix, then test the footage across Meta, TikTok and the product page.

Do you run Facebook ads for Los Angeles brands?

Yes. Meta is usually the main channel, run on broad and Advantage+ campaigns with weekly creative testing and Conversions API tracking.

How much does an ecommerce agency in Los Angeles cost?

Most agencies charge a monthly retainer, a share of ad spend or a mix. We quote after the free audit, and partners typically spend at least $10,000 to $20,000 a month on ads.
