Brand Scaling is a fashion brand ads agency for DTC apparel brands. We run Meta and TikTok ads, creative production, Shopify CRO and email around the way fashion actually sells: in collections, drops and seasons, with sizing questions and returns sitting between the ad and the profit.

Apparel margins are thinner than most DTC categories and return rates are higher, so the job is not just to sell more. It is to sell more of the right size to the right person and protect the margin while doing it. The apparel and fashion case study hub lists the published accounts; here is how we run the work.

What does a fashion brand ads agency do differently?

A fashion brand ads agency plans around collections, drops and seasonal peaks, produces enough creative to keep up with new products, and reduces returns with fit-first content. Brand Scaling runs Meta and TikTok ads for DTC apparel, with results like $59,241 at 3.56x ROAS through BFCM.

What makes fashion ads harder than other categories

A skincare brand might scale one hero product for a year. A fashion brand might launch twelve collections in the same period, each with its own creative needs, inventory limits and sell-through targets. That rhythm breaks a lot of ad accounts, because the learning built on last month's products does not transfer cleanly to this month's.

Returns are the second issue. A sale that comes back is worse than no sale, since you paid for the click, the shipping and the processing. Ads that set the wrong expectation about fit, fabric or colour show up later as returns.

Winning angles and creative for fashion brands

Fashion creative has to do two jobs at once: make the piece desirable and show exactly how it fits. We build around a hook and angle matrix and ship 30 to 50+ new creatives a month, which matters more in apparel than almost anywhere else because the catalog keeps changing.

  • Try-on hauls on different body types, with sizes stated on screen
  • Styling: one piece worn three ways, which also lifts AOV
  • Fabric close-ups: stretch, weight, drape and how it moves
  • Drop urgency: limited runs, restocks and countdowns to launch
  • Creator outfit-of-the-day content run as Spark Ads or partnership ads

Offers and funnel for collections and drops

We avoid discounting the whole catalog, because it trains customers to wait. Instead we use offers that raise average order value and move the right inventory: buy-two bundles, outfit sets, free shipping thresholds and early access for email and SMS subscribers before a drop opens to everyone.

On the product page, fit is the conversion lever. Clear size charts, model measurements, fit notes from reviews and an easy exchange policy raise conversion rate and cut returns at the same time.

Seasonality and BFCM

Fashion revenue clusters around launches, holidays and BFCM. We plan creative and inventory together weeks ahead, warm audiences before the peak and keep budgets flexible through it. A women's fashion brand we run did $59,241 on $16,630 spend at 3.56x ROAS during BFCM, and our BFCM Meta ads playbook covers the week-by-week structure.

Channel mix for apparel brands

Meta carries most apparel acquisition, with catalog ads and broad campaigns working together once a brand has enough products. TikTok is strong for try-on and styling content and for younger buyers. Google Shopping and Performance Max catch people searching for a specific item, and email and SMS drive launch-day revenue from people who already know the brand.

  • Meta: broad campaigns for hero products, catalog retargeting across the range
  • TikTok: creator try-ons, Spark Ads, and TikTok Shop for impulse price points
  • Google: Shopping for named items and colours, branded search during drops
  • Klaviyo: early access lists, back-in-stock alerts and post-purchase styling emails

Measuring fashion ads after returns

In-platform ROAS counts a sale the moment it happens, even if half the order comes back two weeks later. For fashion brands we report net revenue after returns alongside platform numbers, and we look at return rate by product, size and even by ad angle. An angle that sells well but drives a high return rate is not a winner, and cutting it often improves profit more than finding a new hook.

We also track sell-through by collection, so ad spend can move toward stock that needs to clear before the next drop instead of only toward the bestsellers that would sell anyway.

Pricing for fashion ads management

We quote after the audit. Agencies usually charge a monthly retainer, a percentage of ad spend, a performance fee or a mix. For fashion we pay close attention to how a performance fee treats returns, since gross revenue can overstate what the brand keeps. Most fashion partners already spend at least $10K to $20K a month on ads.

Frequently asked questions

What ROAS is good for a fashion brand on Meta?

Our published fashion accounts range from 3.56x to 5.49x ROAS, and seasonal peaks can push numbers higher for a short window. Fashion needs a higher target than some categories because margins are thinner and returns eat into revenue. We set the target from contribution margin after returns, so the ROAS you chase is one that actually leaves profit.

How do fashion brands reduce returns from paid ads?

Start in the creative. Show sizes on different bodies, state the size each model wears and show fabric honestly. Then support it on the product page with clear size charts, fit notes and measurements. Ads that set accurate expectations bring buyers who keep what they order, which matters more to profit than a slightly cheaper click.

How should a fashion brand advertise a new collection drop?

Build anticipation before launch with teasers and an early access list, then open to email and SMS subscribers first. On launch day, run fresh creative to warm audiences and broad campaigns together. After the first few days, move budget toward the pieces selling through fastest and use catalog ads to show the rest of the range.

Are catalog ads or creative ads better for apparel?

Both, for different jobs. Strong video and creator creative wins new customers on hero products, while catalog ads are efficient for retargeting and for showing a wide range. Most apparel accounts we run use broad creative campaigns for acquisition and catalog campaigns for people who have already browsed, with budget split by results.

Should a fashion brand discount for BFCM?

Usually yes, but carefully. Sitewide discounts all year train buyers to wait, so we prefer bundle offers, gifts with purchase or tiered discounts that protect margin. For BFCM itself, a clear and simple offer works best, and planning creative and inventory weeks ahead matters more than the size of the discount.
