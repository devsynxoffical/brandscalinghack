Jewelry Brand Ads That Sell Through Gifting Seasons

Home / Industries / Jewelry Brands

Brand Scaling runs jewelry brand ads for DTC jewelers selling on Shopify, from demi-fine everyday pieces to personalised and higher-priced lines. We manage Meta and TikTok campaigns, creative, product pages and email, with the calendar built around the moments people buy jewelry: Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, graduation, anniversaries and the holiday season.

Jewelry has a useful mix for paid ads: higher average order value than most fashion, strong gifting demand and a product that photographs beautifully when it is shot well. It also has a trust problem, because buyers cannot touch the piece or check the metal before they pay. Our ads and store work are designed around both sides.

How should a DTC jeweler run jewelry brand ads?

Jewelry brand ads work best when creative shows the piece on skin in natural light, campaigns are planned around gifting peaks, and the store answers trust questions about materials, sizing and returns. Brand Scaling runs Meta and TikTok for jewelers, including $54K in 7 days at 4.43x ROAS using manual bidding.

Two buyers: the self-purchaser and the gift giver

Most jewelry brands sell to two very different people. The self-purchaser buys for herself, cares about style, layering and how the piece fits her everyday look, and buys year-round. The gift giver is often buying for a partner or parent, knows little about jewelry, and buys in short windows before a deadline.

We build separate creative and landing paths for each. Self-purchasers respond to styling and stacking content. Gift givers need reassurance: what to pick, whether it will arrive on time, how it is packaged and what happens if it is the wrong size.

Winning angles and creative for jewelry brands

Jewelry creative lives or dies on how the piece looks on a real person. Close-ups on skin, movement in natural light and honest scale (how big the pendant actually is) beat flat lays on velvet. We produce 30 to 50+ new creatives a month from a 45+ cell hook and angle matrix, and for jewelry that matrix leans hard on format testing because small visual details change performance.

  • On-skin close-ups: how the piece catches light during normal movement
  • Stacking and layering: three pieces worn together, which also lifts AOV
  • Gift reaction: the unboxing moment, filmed simply
  • Materials and care: solid gold versus plated, waterproof, hypoallergenic, shown and explained
  • Personalisation: engraving or initials made on camera

Offers and funnel for high AOV and gifting

Because jewelry carries a higher AOV, a single order can absorb a higher acquisition cost. That gives room to retarget over a longer window and to use content like gift guides before the product page. We focus offers on value rather than deep discounts, since heavy discounting makes fine pieces look cheap.

Trust is built on the product page: materials stated plainly, real customer photos, sizing tools for rings, clear delivery cut-off dates before holidays and an easy returns or resizing policy.

Offers we test for jewelry

  • Gift sets and matching pieces at a bundle price
  • Free gift packaging and a handwritten note
  • Spend thresholds that add a small free piece
  • Guaranteed delivery messaging in the final week before a gifting date

Bidding and seasonality

Gifting peaks compress demand into a few weeks, and costs rise as other brands crowd in. We plan creative weeks ahead, build warm audiences before each peak and control spend tightly once auction prices climb. For one jewelry brand we used manual bidding campaigns, with cost controls instead of pure automated bidding, and drove $54K in 7 days at 4.43x ROAS. Manual bidding is not right for every account, but in jewelry, where order values are high and peaks are short, it can stop the algorithm from overpaying during the busiest days.

Channel mix for jewelry brands

Meta does most of the work for jewelry acquisition, especially Instagram placements where jewelry content looks native. TikTok works well for styling and personalisation content and for younger self-purchasers. Google Shopping matters more for jewelry than many categories because gift givers search for specific items like "gold initial necklace" close to the deadline. Email and SMS handle gifting reminders, birthday flows and repeat buyers who collect pieces over time.

We also use customer data from gifting peaks carefully. Someone who bought a necklace as a Valentine's gift is likely to buy again before the next anniversary or holiday, so we tag gift purchases in Klaviyo and send reminders before the dates that matter. For self-purchasers, new collection launches and matching pieces to what they already own do the same job, turning one order into a collection.

Frequently asked questions

What ROAS can a jewelry brand get from Meta ads?

Our published jewelry case reached 4.43x ROAS, with $54K in 7 days using manual bidding. Results vary by price point and season. Jewelry's higher AOV means you can often accept a higher cost per purchase than a lower-priced product, so we set targets from your margin per order rather than chasing a ROAS figure that may leave growth on the table.

When should jewelry brands start holiday advertising?

Earlier than most expect. We start building warm audiences and testing gift creative several weeks before each peak, so proven ads are ready when costs rise. For the holiday season that means testing in October, and for Valentine's Day it means early January. Delivery cut-off dates should appear in ads during the final week.

Is manual bidding better than automated bidding for jewelry?

Not always. Manual bidding with cost caps or bid caps helped one jewelry brand reach $54K in 7 days at 4.43x ROAS, because it stopped overpaying during expensive peak days. But it can restrict delivery if caps are set too low. We usually test it against automated bidding once the account has enough purchase data.

How do you build trust for an online jewelry brand?

Show the piece honestly on real people, state the metal and plating clearly, and add customer photos, ring sizing tools and a simple returns or resizing policy to the product page. Packaging and delivery promises matter for gifts. These elements raise conversion rate, which lowers your cost per purchase as much as better targeting does.

Does Google Shopping work for jewelry brands?

Often yes, especially for gift buyers searching for specific items close to a deadline. Clean product titles with metal, style and personalisation details help. We usually run Meta as the main acquisition channel and add Google Shopping or Performance Max once product feeds and tracking are set up, measuring both by blended MER.
