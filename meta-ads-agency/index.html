Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Meta ads agency improve my ecommerce ROAS?

Usually, if the account has room in creative, structure or tracking. The biggest ROAS improvements we see come from more and better creative, consolidating into fewer broad campaigns, fixing Conversions API tracking and raising order value with bundles. We start every engagement with an audit that shows where your ROAS is being lost.

What does a Meta ads agency do for an ecommerce brand?

A Meta ads agency plans, builds and runs Facebook and Instagram campaigns that are measured on revenue, cost per acquisition and ROAS. Brand Scaling also produces the direct-response video creative, validates Conversions API tracking and ties the ads to the Shopify store, because media buying alone cannot fix a weak offer or a slow product page.

How much does a Meta ads agency cost?

Most ecommerce Meta ads agencies charge a monthly retainer, a percentage of ad spend, or a mix of both. Brand Scaling works with brands spending at least $10,000 to $20,000 per month on paid ads, and every engagement starts with a Brand Growth Audit so you see the plan and the numbers before any fee is agreed.

What is a good ROAS for Meta ads?

A good ROAS depends on gross margin. A store with 60% margin breaks even at 1.67x ROAS, a store with 40% margin needs 2.5x just to break even. Our client accounts typically run between 3x and 6x on scale, with top accounts such as $2.3M in sales at 11.6x ROAS on $200K spend. Use the free ROAS calculator to find your own break-even number.

Meta ads agency vs an in-house media buyer: which is better?

An in-house buyer gives you one person and one skill set. An agency gives you media buying, creative production, CRO and tracking in one team, plus benchmarks from dozens of accounts in your niche. For brands under about $2M a month in revenue, an agency usually reaches profitable scale faster and costs less than hiring a full team.

How do you scale DTC brands with retargeting after iOS 14?

We keep retargeting small and let Advantage+ shopping campaigns and catalog ads do most of the work. Retargeting budgets run at roughly 10% to 15% of spend, use dynamic product ads for site visitors and cart abandoners, and are measured against blended MER rather than platform-reported ROAS, so the account does not double count the same customers.

How long until a new Meta ads account shows results?

The first 7 to 14 days are foundation work: tracking, offer, product page and the first creative batch. Most accounts see a clear winning angle inside the first 30 days and reach a stable scaling budget by day 60 to 90, depending on margin and creative volume.

Do you only work with Shopify stores?

Shopify is the platform most of our clients use and the one our CRO team builds on, but the media buying and creative systems work for any DTC store with a tracked checkout. The requirement is product-market fit and enough margin to scale, not a specific platform.

Which bid strategy we use, and when

New tests start on lowest cost bidding while we learn what works. Once cost per purchase is stable, cost controls come in. Bid caps and cost caps held a skincare account at 4.39x ROAS and a women's fashion account at 3.56x through BFCM, and a jewelry brand drove $54K in 7 days on manual bid caps.
