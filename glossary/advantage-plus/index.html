Advantage Plus Shopping Campaigns: What They Are and How They Work

Home / Glossary / Advantage+ Shopping

By Gaurav Kapoor, Founder, Brand Scaling · Updated 2026-11-05

Advantage plus shopping is the common name for Meta's Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns, an automated campaign type built for ecommerce sales. Meta has since folded them into Advantage+ sales campaigns, but the idea is the same: fewer manual controls, more machine learning, and a campaign that finds buyers across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and the Audience Network on its own.

For DTC brands, Advantage+ often becomes the main scaling campaign. The trade-off is less control over who sees your ads in exchange for broader reach and less time spent managing ad sets.

What are Advantage plus shopping campaigns?

Advantage plus shopping campaigns are Meta's automated campaign type for online sales, now part of Advantage+ sales campaigns. You supply budget, creative and a conversion goal, and Meta handles targeting, placements and much of the optimisation using machine learning. They work best for brands with reliable purchase tracking and a steady flow of strong creative.

Advantage+ Shopping (Advantage+ sales campaigns)
Advantage+ shopping campaigns are Meta's automated ecommerce sales campaigns that use machine learning to handle targeting, placements and creative delivery.

How Advantage+ shopping campaigns work

Instead of building ad sets around interests or lookalikes, you set a country, budget, conversion event and a pool of creative. Meta's system decides who to show each ad to, which placement to use and how to spread spend across the creative. Your creative effectively becomes your targeting, because the people who respond to each ad teach the algorithm who to find next.

You can tell Meta who your existing customers are, usually through a customer list or pixel data, and set an existing customer budget cap. Worked example (illustrative numbers): on a $1,000 daily budget with a 20% cap, at most $200 a day can go to people who already bought, leaving at least $800 for new customer acquisition.

These campaigns rely on purchase data. With a weak pixel and no Conversions API, the algorithm has little to learn from.

What good Advantage+ results depend on

There is no fixed ROAS that Advantage+ will deliver. Results depend on the same fundamentals as any Meta campaign, with automation amplifying both strengths and weaknesses. Across our 25 published case studies, ROAS ranged from 2.06x to 11.6x with a median of 3.97x, which shows how much the product, offer and creative matter. The main factors are:

  • Tracking quality: clean purchase events through pixel and Conversions API.
  • Creative volume and variety: enough distinct angles and formats for Meta to test.
  • Offer and landing page: automation cannot fix a page that does not convert.
  • Budget: enough spend for the campaign to collect conversions and stabilise.
  • Existing customer settings: without a cap, spend can drift toward easy repeat buyers.

How to get more from Advantage+

Feed it creative. Because targeting is automated, the main way to steer the campaign is through what you put into it. Add new angles and hooks regularly and remove ads that have stopped earning delivery. A mix of UGC, static and demonstration videos gives the system more ways to reach different buyers.

Set the existing customer budget cap so you can see how much of the result comes from new customers. Avoid frequent edits that restart learning. Scale budget in steps, often 20% to 30% at a time, and watch blended numbers in Shopify rather than only in-platform ROAS. Many brands run Advantage+ alongside a manual testing campaign, then move proven winners into Advantage+ to scale. Our Advantage+ guide covers setup in more detail.

Common Advantage+ mistakes

  • Launching with only two or three creatives, which gives the algorithm too little to work with.
  • Leaving the existing customer cap off and celebrating ROAS driven by repeat buyers.
  • Running it on a pixel with missing or duplicated purchase events.
  • Changing budget and creative every day, so the campaign never stabilises.
  • Judging it only by Meta-reported ROAS instead of blended ROAS and new customer revenue.

Related terms

Advantage+ depends on accurate data, so the Conversions API is a near prerequisite. Creative fatigue matters more in automated campaigns, because creative is your main control. Blended ROAS and MER are the best way to check whether Advantage+ is adding real revenue. Our Meta ads agency page explains how we structure accounts around these campaigns.

One practical check is worth building into your weekly review. Compare the new customer revenue reported in Shopify with what the Advantage+ campaign claims, and watch how the share of spend going to existing customers changes over time. If reported ROAS rises while new customer counts stay flat, the campaign is likely harvesting buyers you already had rather than growing the brand.

Frequently asked questions

Are Advantage plus shopping campaigns still available?

Meta has merged Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns into a broader Advantage+ sales campaign setup, so you may now see the automation applied when you choose a sales objective rather than as a separate campaign type. The core features remain: automated targeting and placements, creative testing within one campaign and controls for existing customer spend.

Do Advantage+ campaigns replace manual campaigns?

Not entirely. Many DTC brands use Advantage+ as the main scaling campaign and keep a manual campaign for structured creative testing, offers or specific audiences. Proven winners from testing can then move into Advantage+. The right split depends on budget, catalogue and how much control you need over who sees which message.

How much budget does Advantage+ need?

It needs enough budget to generate a steady flow of purchases so the system can learn. If daily spend only buys one or two conversions, results will swing a lot. Brands with very small budgets may do better consolidating into a single campaign until volume grows. Use a budget calculator to estimate conversions from a given spend.

What is the existing customer budget cap?

It is a setting that limits how much of an Advantage+ budget can be spent on people Meta identifies as existing customers. You define existing customers with customer lists or website data. Setting a cap pushes more spend toward new customer acquisition and makes results easier to interpret, since repeat buyers often inflate ROAS.

Can Advantage+ work for a new brand?

It can, but it works best once the pixel and Conversions API are reporting purchases reliably and you have several strong creatives. For a brand with little purchase history, the algorithm has less to learn from at first. Getting tracking, offer and product page right in the first weeks usually matters more than the campaign type.
