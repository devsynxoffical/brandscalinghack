What does an ecommerce CRO agency do?

An ecommerce CRO agency finds why visitors leave without buying and tests fixes. We study analytics, heatmaps and customer questions, then improve speed, product pages, offers, landing pages and checkout one test at a time.

Research before redesign

We never start with a redesign. We start with data: where visitors drop off, what they ask before buying and what stops mobile shoppers at checkout.

  • Funnel data from Shopify and analytics: product view to add to cart, cart to checkout, checkout to purchase, split by device and traffic source.
  • Session recordings and heatmaps on the top landing pages from paid ads.
  • Customer reviews, support tickets and post-purchase survey answers, read for objections and the words buyers use.
  • Ad comments and the hooks that win on Meta and TikTok, so the page matches what brought people in.
  • A speed and app audit of the theme on real mobile devices.

Speed and product pages

Paid social traffic is almost all mobile and very impatient. We remove unused apps, compress media, defer scripts that block the first view and simplify the theme where it is heavy. Speed work is often the cheapest conversion gain available.

On the product page, we focus on the first screen. The visitor should see the product, what it does, the price and the offer, and the add to cart button without scrolling. Below that, we order the content by the objections we found in research: proof, how it works, ingredients or materials, sizing, shipping and returns. UGC clips and before and after images from the ads often belong on the page too.

Offers, bundles and order value

A store that converts at 3% instead of 2% can afford to pay much more per click and keep the same margin. Bundles, thresholds and post-purchase offers raise AOV so every ad dollar works harder.

Post-purchase upsells shown after payment and before the thank-you page are one of the most reliable AOV gains on Shopify, because they add revenue without adding friction to the first purchase. We set them up with products that pair naturally with what was just bought.

Landing pages matched to the ad angle

When an ad promises one benefit and lands on a generic product page, people bounce. We build landing pages that continue the exact hook of the ad.

We map each winning ad angle to a landing experience. Sometimes that is the product page with a different first screen, sometimes a dedicated landing page or advertorial for colder audiences, and sometimes a bundle page for an offer-led ad. Because our creative team ships new angles every week, landing pages are updated on the same rhythm, not once a year.

We judge these pages on revenue per visitor from the ad that feeds them, not on site-wide averages. A page can lift conversion for one angle and do nothing for another, so the read has to be specific to the traffic source.

Checkout and A/B testing

Shopify checkout is already well built, so the wins here are usually about removing doubt: clear shipping costs and delivery times before checkout, express payment options visible, trust signals near the button, and a cart drawer that does not push people back into browsing.

For testing, we run one clear hypothesis at a time on high-traffic pages, with a stated metric and a minimum sample before calling a result. Lower-traffic stores test bigger changes, since small tweaks need far more visitors to read. Every test, win or lose, goes into a log so learning compounds instead of being repeated.

How a CRO engagement runs

For brands that need dedicated funnels or advertorial landing pages for cold traffic, we build those too, using the frameworks from our sister site FunnelsThatPrint.

  • Weeks 1 to 2: research, speed audit, funnel benchmarks and a ranked list of fixes.
  • Weeks 2 to 4: quick wins shipped, such as speed fixes, first-screen product page changes and clearer shipping messages.
  • Month 2 onward: a steady testing rhythm on product pages, offers and landing pages, with results reported as revenue per visitor and conversion rate.
  • Ongoing: new landing pages matched to new ad angles as the creative team ships them.

Frequently asked questions

What is ecommerce CRO?

Ecommerce CRO (conversion rate optimization) is the work of turning more store visitors into buyers and raising order value. It uses analytics, heatmaps and customer research to find what stops people buying, then tests fixes to speed, product pages, offers and checkout. One clinical skincare store we work with reached a 5.25% conversion rate.

What is a good ecommerce conversion rate?

It depends on price point and traffic source. One clinical skincare store we work with reached 5.25% in a $61,000 month, but high-ticket stores convert lower and can still be very profitable.

What is included in an ecommerce CRO audit?

Analytics review, funnel drop-off analysis, speed checks, product page and checkout review, and a ranked list of tests by expected impact.

How long does CRO take to show results?

Quick fixes like speed and checkout friction can show within weeks. A/B tests need enough traffic to reach a clear result, usually two to four weeks each.

Do I need a CRO agency if I run ads?

If you spend heavily on ads, yes. Every lift in conversion rate lowers your cost per purchase on all traffic at once.

Do you do CRO for Shopify stores?

Yes. Most of our CRO work is on Shopify and Shopify Plus.
