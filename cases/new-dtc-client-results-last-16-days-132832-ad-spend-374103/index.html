DTC Ecommerce Case Study: $374,103 in 16 Days at 2.82x ROAS on $132,832 Ad Spend

In a 16-day window a DTC client spent $132,832 on Meta ads and generated $374,103 in revenue across 6,687 units, a 2.82x ROAS at very high volume, five months into the engagement and before heavy optimisation. The breakdown credits a real offer, scroll-stopping creative and patience with the system over discount gimmicks.

Write-up by Gaurav Kapoor, Founder, Brand Scaling · Updated 2026-11-03

What We Did

  • Replaced discount-led ads with a clear offer and creatives built to stop the scroll.
  • Held the campaign structure steady instead of reacting to daily fluctuations.
  • Scaled budget in steps while keeping creative fresh so delivery never collapsed.

Result: $374,103 revenue on $132,832 spend in 16 days, 6,687 units, 2.82x ROAS at scale.

Key Takeaways

  • Just a real offer, scroll-breaking creatives, and a scaling system that prints consistent results.
  • Most brands throw money at ads, burn out their creatives in 3 days, and blame the algorithm when nothing converts.
