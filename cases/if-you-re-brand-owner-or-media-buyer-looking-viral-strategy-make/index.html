Fashion Case Study: $79,859 in 1 Day at 4.41x ROAS

A sneaker brand made $79,859 in sales in one day from a pre-Christmas sale, at a 4.41 ROAS, which implies roughly $18,100 of ad spend ($79,859 divided by 4.41). The post explains that the work started before the sale opened. The offer was teased by email and ads three days ahead, the best customers got early VIP access, and more than 300 VIPs were ready to buy at launch. On the day, a 24-hour flash sale with a countdown timer ran alongside gift-led ads and abandoned-cart retargeting.

The Challenge

The post points out that most brands only start promoting once a sale is already live, which leaves no built-up demand on launch day.

The Approach

Build demand before the sale, then sell the sneakers as a memorable Christmas gift, selling a feeling rather than a product, with scarcity and urgency on the day itself.

What We Did

  • Teased the offer by email and ads three days before launch, with lines such as "The biggest drop of the season is almost here… Are you ready?"
  • Gave the best customers early VIP access, so more than 300 VIPs were ready to purchase when the sale started.
  • Ran gift-led ads: the hook "What's the one gift everyone will remember this Christmas? These sneakers.", a nostalgic unboxing video and UGC-style testimonials.
  • Targeted warm audiences from past purchases and engagement, plus lookalike audiences built from the VIP buyers.
  • Ran a 24-hour flash sale with limited discounts and a countdown timer on every product page, and retargeted abandoned carts.

Result: $79,859 in sales in one day at a 4.41 ROAS (about $18,100 implied ad spend), sneaker brand, pre-Christmas sale.

Most brands wait until the sale goes live to start promoting. That’s a rookie move.

Here’s what we did instead:

Result? By the time the sale started, we had 300+ VIPs ready to purchase.
