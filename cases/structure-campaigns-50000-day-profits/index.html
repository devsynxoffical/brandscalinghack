DTC Ecommerce Case Study: $66,168 in 1 Day on $19,118 Ad Spend

In a single day, a client spent $19,118 on ads and made back $66,168, a return of about 3.46x ($66,168 divided by $19,118), or $47,050 of revenue above ad spend. The post is a how-to for the campaign structure behind it. It uses a full funnel of prospecting, warm retargeting and high-intent retargeting, Advantage+ shopping campaigns that start small and grow gradually, and a move to manual bidding once the pixel has enough purchase data. It gives concrete thresholds for each step, listed below.

The Approach

A three-layer funnel (top, middle and bottom), Advantage+ shopping campaigns scaled in small steps, and manual bidding with a bid cap set slightly above the average cost per purchase once the pixel has 300 to 400 conversions.

What We Did

  • Top of funnel: targeted new, high-intent audiences (lookalikes and broad interests) in ABO campaigns with at least $10 per ad set.
  • Middle and bottom of funnel: retargeted add-to-carts and 50%+ video viewers, then past purchasers and 75%+ video viewers.
  • Started Advantage+ shopping campaigns at $100 a day and raised budgets by 20% to 30% every 3 to 5 days as performance stabilised.
  • After 300 to 400 conversions, switched to manual bidding with a bid cap slightly above average cost per purchase, for example $30 to $35 on a $25 average.

Result: $66,168 in revenue on $19,118 of ad spend in one day (about 3.46x).

Key Takeaways

  • Start with a Strong Foundation: Campaign Structure:
  • Top-of-Funnel (TOF): Target new, high-intent audiences (Lookalikes, broad interests) **ABO with atleast $10Per Adset Min.**
  • Middle-of-Funnel (MOF): Retarget warm audiences (Add-to-Cart, 50%+ video viewers).
  • Bottom-of-Funnel (BOF): Engage your most high-intent audience (past purchasers, 75%+ video watchers).

How to Structure Campaigns for $50,000+/Day Profits

Want to scale your campaigns to $50K+ per day in profit, just like we did for one of our clients who spent $19,118 and made back $66,168 in a single day?

Here’s the exact structure we used to make it happen:

Start with a Strong Foundation: Campaign Structure:

Top-of-Funnel (TOF): Target new, high-intent audiences (Lookalikes, broad interests) **ABO with atleast $10Per Adset Min.**

Middle-of-Funnel (MOF): Retarget warm audiences (Add-to-Cart, 50%+ video viewers).

Bottom-of-Funnel (BOF): Engage your most high-intent audience (past purchasers, 75%+ video watchers).

Utilize Advantage+ Campaigns (ASC)

Start at $100/day for testing, then gradually scale by 20%-30% every 3-5 days as performance stabilizes. Be patient—this takes time, but the rewards are worth it.

Aggressively Scaling with Manual Bidding

Once your pixel has gathered enough purchase data (300-400 conversions), Facebook’s algorithm has a solid understanding of your audience.

Go to Manual Bidding and set your bid cap slightly higher than your average Cost Per Purchase (CPP).

Let's say, If your average CPP is $25, set your bid cap at $30-$35 to ensure you're competitive in the auction without overspending.
