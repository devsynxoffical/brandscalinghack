DTC Ecommerce Case Study: $64,000 in 24 Days

The brand was onboarded in the last week of November 2023, when it was doing under $20,000 a month and had worked with several other agencies and media buyers. In the first 24 days of January 2024 it crossed $64,000 from 316 orders, an average order value of about $200 ($64,000 divided by 316 is $202.53). The post reports a 38% revenue increase, a store conversion rate that almost doubled, a 50% drop in CPA and a 50% rise in AOV. The work covered the account structure, the store design, new ads and upsell offers.

The Challenge

The brand could not scale past $20,000 a month, its winning ads fatigued whenever spend was pushed, average order value was under $100, and the conversion rate never passed 1.5%, which kept CPA high.

The Approach

Rebuild from the ground up across ads and store at the same time: restructure audiences, extend proven creative concepts, improve the store's appeal and lift order value with upsells.

What We Did

  • Restructured the account's audiences and segmented the winning creatives.
  • Created new ads based on the same concepts as the existing winning creatives.
  • Reworked the store design to make the offers more appealing.
  • Added supplementary products as new upsell offers.

Result: $64,000 from 316 orders in 24 days of January 2024 (about $200 AOV), conversion rate almost doubled, CPA down 50%, AOV up 50%.
