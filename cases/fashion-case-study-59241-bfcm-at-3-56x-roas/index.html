Fashion Case Study: $59,241 at 3.56x ROAS on $16,630 Ad Spend

A women's fashion brand generated $59,241 from $16,630 of ad spend during BFCM, a 3.56 ROAS, with the post noting the result held through the last day of November. Holiday conditions made this harder than usual: CPMs spike, bid caps refuse to spend and creative fatigues faster. The post credits cost-controlled campaigns (bid caps and cost caps) run alongside Advantage+ shopping campaigns, a high volume of fresh creative aimed at holiday shoppers, and daily audits that scaled winners fast and cut underperformers before they drained the budget.

The Challenge

During BFCM, CPMs spike, bid caps can stop spending and creative fatigue sets in faster than at any other time of year.

The Approach

Fresh creative at high volume for holiday shoppers: eye-catching visuals, relatable messaging and offers built for the season, replacing ads before they fatigued.

What We Did

  • Combined cost-controlled campaigns (bid caps and cost caps) with Advantage+ shopping campaigns to protect profit in a volatile auction.
  • Launched a steady stream of fresh holiday creatives so ads were replaced before fatigue set in.
  • Audited every campaign daily, scaling winners quickly and switching off underperformers.

Result: $59,241 in revenue on $16,630 of ad spend during BFCM, 3.56 ROAS, women's fashion brand.

Key Takeaways

  • Mastering Creative Velocity: Instead of letting ads fatigue, we unleashed a storm of fresh creatives—eye-catching visuals, relatable messaging, and irresistible offers tailored for holiday shoppers.
  • Ruthless Optimization: Every campaign was audited daily, with quick pivots based on performance—scaling winners fast and pulling the plug on underperformers before they drained budgets.

Here’s what we did differently to keep scaling this women’s fashion brand with insane ROAS

Taming the Meta Beast: Cost-controlled campaigns (bid caps + cost caps) plus ASC campaigns ensured profitability while giving Meta the room to work its magic, even in volatile conditions.

Mastering Creative Velocity: Instead of letting ads fatigue, we unleashed a storm of fresh creatives—eye-catching visuals, relatable messaging, and irresistible offers tailored for holiday shoppers.

Ruthless Optimization: Every campaign was audited daily, with quick pivots based on performance—scaling winners fast and pulling the plug on underperformers before they drained budgets.
