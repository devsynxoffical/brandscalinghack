Who our DTC performance marketing services are for

Our DTC performance marketing services are built for brands that already sell. Most partners do somewhere between $20K and $2M+ a month in revenue, have product-market fit, and spend at least $10K to $20K a month on paid ads or are ready to. We work with brands in the US, the UK, Canada and Australia, mostly on Shopify.

Below that level the problem is usually finding a product or an offer that sells, and an agency retainer is rarely the best use of the money. Above it, the problem shifts to keeping efficiency as spend grows: more creative, a store that converts colder traffic, and retention that lifts lifetime value so the ad account can pay more for each new customer. That second problem is the one these services are designed for.

How an engagement runs, from audit to scale

Every engagement starts with a Brand Growth Audit: a 1-on-1 review with Gaurav Kapoor of your ad account, creative, store and unit economics. The output is a plan that names the two or three constraints holding growth back and the order to fix them in. You see it before any fee is agreed.

From there the work follows four phases. The timings are typical, not fixed: a brand with clean tracking and a strong offer moves through Foundation faster, and a brand with thin margins may spend longer in Optimize before budget goes up.

How DTC performance marketing services are priced

Agencies in this space usually charge in one of four ways: a fixed monthly retainer, a percentage of ad spend, a performance fee tied to revenue or profit, or a mix of a base fee and a performance component. Each has a trade-off. A retainer is predictable but does not move with results. A percentage of spend rewards spending more, which is not always what the brand needs. A pure performance fee aligns incentives but depends on both sides agreeing what counts as performance.

Brand Scaling quotes after the audit, because the right scope depends on which services the brand needs and how much creative it will take. When you compare quotes from any agency, check three things: what creative volume is included, who owns the ad accounts and the store code, and which number the agency reports on. An agency judged on platform ROAS alone can look good while blended profit falls.

More questions

Which service should a DTC brand start with?

Start where the biggest constraint is. If tracking is broken, every other decision is built on bad data, so that comes first. If the ads work but stop working after a few weeks, the constraint is creative volume. If clicks are cheap but sales are not, it is the store or the offer. The Brand Growth Audit is how we find which one applies.

Can I hire Brand Scaling for one channel only?

Yes. Some brands start with Meta ads or a Shopify rebuild alone. We will tell you during the audit if another part of the system is likely to cap results, for example a product page that cannot convert more traffic, but the scope is your decision. Many partners add services once the first channel is working and the next constraint shows up.
