This TikTok Shop break even ROAS calculator shows what you keep from each order after TikTok and your creators take their share, and the lowest return your TikTok ads or GMV Max campaigns can run at without losing money. It also works as a TikTok Shop profit calculator and fees calculator: enter your price, product cost, shipping, referral fee and affiliate commission, and it returns profit per order before ads, total fees and commission, margin, break-even ROAS and the most you can pay in ads for one order.

TikTok Shop costs stack up faster than on your own Shopify store. The platform takes a referral fee on every sale, creators often take a commission on top, and ads come after both. Run the numbers per order first, then set your ad targets from the result.

How do I calculate break-even ROAS for TikTok Shop?

Take your selling price and subtract product cost, shipping, the TikTok Shop referral fee and any affiliate commission. That is your profit per order before ads. Divide the price by it. A $35 order with $8 COGS, $6 shipping, an 8% example referral fee and 15% affiliate commission leaves $12.95, so break-even ROAS is 2.70x.

How to use the TikTok Shop profit calculator

Use per-order figures for a typical order, not monthly totals. Fees and commission are calculated as a percentage of the selling price, so the calculator adds them up for you.

A note on the referral fee: TikTok Shop rates vary by region, category and date. TikTok's US Seller University fee table listed a 6% referral fee for most categories, in effect since April 1, 2024, with a few jewelry and pre-owned categories lower. Several seller guides published in 2026 report a rise to 8% for most US categories from August 2026, but we could not confirm that figure on an official TikTok page as of November 2026. The calculator uses 8% as an example rate. Check Seller Center for the rate on your category and enter that.

  • Selling price per order: what the customer pays for a typical order, before tax.
  • Product cost (COGS) per order: the landed cost of the goods, including supplier price, inbound freight and duties.
  • Shipping and fulfilment you pay: postage, packaging and any 3PL or pick and pack fees that come out of your pocket. If TikTok or the buyer covers shipping, enter only your share.
  • TikTok Shop referral fee %: the platform fee for your category, taken from Seller Center.
  • Affiliate commission %: the rate you set for creators in your affiliate plan. If only some orders come through creators, enter a blended rate. For example, 40% of orders at 15% commission is 6%.
  • Other fee %: any payment or extra platform fee your region charges separately. Leave it at 0 if your Seller Center statement shows none.

The formula with a worked example

The calculator works out what one order leaves you before ads, then turns that into a ROAS floor.

Fees and commission = price x (referral % + affiliate % + other %)

Profit per order before ads = price - COGS - shipping - fees and commission

Break-even ROAS = price / profit per order before ads

Break-even CPA = profit per order before ads

Margin before ads = profit per order before ads / price

Example (illustrative numbers, not client data): a $35 order with $8 COGS and $6 shipping and fulfilment. At an 8% example referral fee, TikTok takes $2.80. A 15% affiliate commission costs $5.25. With no other fee, fees and commission total $8.05. Profit per order before ads is $35 - $8 - $6 - $8.05 = $12.95, a 37.0% margin. Break-even ROAS is $35 / $12.95 = 2.70x, and you can spend up to $12.95 in ads to win one order before you lose money.

How to read the result

Break-even ROAS is your floor, not your goal. If your GMV Max campaign or TikTok ads deliver a ROAS above it, each ad-driven order makes money before overheads. Below it, every order you buy with ads loses money, even if revenue looks strong in Seller Center. GMV Max asks for a ROI target, which is gross revenue divided by ad spend, so you can compare it directly with the break-even ROAS here.

Two things change the answer more than people expect. First, affiliate commission: an order that comes through a creator video you also boost pays both the commission and the ad cost, so include the commission when you set ad targets for those orders. If your ad-driven orders pay no commission, run the calculator with affiliate at 0 for your ad floor. Second, price: a low-priced product leaves little room once fixed costs like shipping are paid, which is why bundles often make TikTok Shop maths work.

If the calculator shows No margin, your fees, commission and costs already use up the whole price, and no ad result can fix that.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good break-even ROAS for TikTok Shop?

There is no single good number, because it depends on your price, costs, referral fee and affiliate commission. A lower break-even ROAS is better, because it gives your ads more room. In our example it is 2.70x. Work out your own figure with the calculator, then set your GMV Max ROI target above it.

How much does TikTok Shop take per sale?

TikTok charges a referral fee as a percentage of each sale, and the rate depends on your region and category. TikTok's US fee table listed 6% for most categories from April 2024, and 2026 seller guides report 8% from August 2026, which we could not confirm officially. Seller Center shows the current rate for your category. Affiliate commission, if you use creators, is on top.

Should I include affiliate commission in my break-even ROAS?

Include it for any order that pays both commission and ad cost, such as a boosted creator video. Leave it out for ad orders that pay no commission. If you are not sure, enter a blended rate based on the share of orders that come through creators.

How is TikTok Shop break-even ROAS different from my Shopify break-even ROAS?

The formula is the same, price divided by profit per order before ads, but TikTok Shop adds a referral fee and often a creator commission. That usually means less profit per order and a higher break-even ROAS for the same product.

What does break-even CPA mean on TikTok Shop?

It is the most you can spend on ads to win one order without losing money. It equals your profit per order before ads. In the example, that is $12.95. If your cost per purchase is higher than that, each ad-driven order loses money.
