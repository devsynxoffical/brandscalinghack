What is DTC affiliate program management?

DTC affiliate program management means an outside team runs your affiliate and creator programme for you: recruiting partners, setting commissions, tracking sales, paying out, checking for fraud and turning the best affiliate content into paid ads. You get a channel paid mostly on results without having to staff it in-house.

What we manage for you

The work splits into five areas. Each one is a weekly task, not a setup step, which is why most in-house programmes slip.

Recruiting

We source creators, reviewers, niche publishers and existing customers who fit your product. Outreach is personal, with a clear offer and a sample where it makes sense. We also convert your best customers and reviewers into affiliates, since they already believe in the product.

Commissions and offers

Commission rates are set from your contribution margin, with tiers that reward partners who drive more sales. We decide when to use a flat fee for content, when to pay a percentage, and when a discount code for the audience works better than a higher commission for the creator.

Tracking and attribution

We set up tracking links and unique codes through your affiliate platform and Shopify, check that orders attribute correctly, and agree attribution rules with you upfront so partners and paid channels are not both credited for the same sale without you knowing.

Payouts

We review commissions each cycle, remove returns and cancelled orders, approve payouts and keep partners informed. Paying accurately and on time is a large part of keeping good affiliates active.

Fraud and code leakage

We watch for self-referrals, coupon sites posting partner codes, trademark bidding on your brand name, and sudden spikes from unknown sources. Problem partners are removed and leaked codes are replaced.

Turning affiliate content into paid ads

The most useful thing an affiliate gives a DTC brand is often not the sale, it is the content. A creator video that already sells to their audience is proven creative. With the creator's permission, we run it as a Meta partnership ad from their handle or as a TikTok Spark Ad on their original post.

This is where our affiliate service ties into everything else we do. Our paid team already ships 30 to 50+ new creatives a month for partner brands, and affiliate content feeds straight into that testing. Winners get budget, and the hooks that work get briefed back to other affiliates so the whole programme improves. If your affiliates mainly sell through TikTok Shop, that side is covered on our TikTok Shop agency page at /tiktok-shop-agency.

How the first 90 days run

  • Days 1 to 14: margin model and commission structure, affiliate platform and tracking set up or audited, partner terms written, first recruitment list built.
  • Days 15 to 45: outreach and sample waves, onboarding kits with product facts, hooks and do and do not guidance, first content reviewed for ad use.
  • Days 45 to 90: commissions adjusted by partner performance, top content tested as partnership ads and Spark Ads, inactive partners re-engaged or removed.
  • Day 90 onward: recruitment scaled toward partner profiles that sell, monthly reporting on affiliate revenue, cost per order and content used in ads.

Partner terms and platform set-up

A programme is only as manageable as its rules. Before recruiting anyone, we write partner terms that cover commission rates and tiers, the cookie or attribution window, what counts as a valid sale, how returns are handled, where codes may and may not be posted, whether bidding on your brand name is allowed, and how long you can use their content in ads.

We work with the affiliate or creator platform you already have where it does the job, or help you choose one that connects cleanly to Shopify, issues unique links and codes, and handles payouts. The aim is that a new partner can be approved, onboarded and tracked in minutes, and that every order in the affiliate report can be matched to a real Shopify order. Clear terms also make the difficult conversations easier, since removing a partner who breaks them is simply applying what both sides agreed.

Who this is for

Affiliate management fits brands with a product creators are happy to talk about and margin to share. Skincare, supplements, fitness, fashion and accessories are natural fits, along with any product with a clear before and after or a daily routine. Brands typically come to us doing about $20K to $2M+ a month and already running paid ads, so the content has somewhere to go.

It is less suited to very low-margin products, where commissions eat the profit, or to brands without enough stock to support a spike from a creator post. If you are not sure which side you fall on, the audit will show whether the margin and stock position can carry a commission-based channel before you recruit a single partner.

Agencies that run affiliate programmes usually charge a monthly management retainer, a percentage of affiliate revenue, a performance fee, or a mix. Commissions, samples and affiliate platform subscriptions are paid by the brand on top, so compare quotes on total cost per order.

Frequently asked questions

What does an affiliate program manager do?

An affiliate program manager recruits and onboards partners, sets commission rates, makes sure tracking credits the right partner, approves and sends payouts, and watches for fraud and leaked codes. In our service the manager also reviews affiliate content for use in paid ads, so the programme feeds your creative testing.

How much commission should a DTC affiliate program pay?

Base it on contribution margin per order after product, shipping and fees, then decide what share you can give away and still profit. Tiered rates work well: a standard rate for everyone and higher rates for partners who sell consistently. Paying a flat content fee plus a lower commission suits some creators better.

How do you stop affiliate codes leaking to coupon sites?

Use unique codes per partner, set clear terms that ban posting codes on coupon and deal sites, and monitor where orders using each code come from. When a code leaks, replace it and talk to the partner. Some brands also limit codes to first orders or set expiry dates for campaign codes.

Can affiliate videos be used as Facebook ads?

Yes, with the creator's permission. Meta partnership ads run the content from the creator's handle, which often reads as more trustworthy than a brand post. On TikTok, Spark Ads do the same with the original post. Agree usage rights and duration in your affiliate terms before running the content.

Is affiliate management different from influencer marketing?

They overlap. Affiliate programmes pay mainly on commission and scale through many partners. Influencer marketing usually means fixed fees for planned content from fewer, larger creators. Many brands use both, with influencer work covered on our influencer marketing agency page and affiliates handled as an always-on channel.
