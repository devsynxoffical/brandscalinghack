What does a Shopify marketing agency do?

A Shopify marketing agency brings buyers to your Shopify store and turns them into repeat customers. It runs paid ads on Meta, TikTok and Google, produces and tests the ad creative, sets up email and SMS in Klaviyo, and fixes the store pages that lose sales. A good one reports on revenue, new-customer cost and profit from Shopify data, not on clicks.

What we measure for Shopify brands

Platform dashboards each claim credit for the same sale, so we report from Shopify first and use ad platform numbers to manage campaigns.

  • MER: total Shopify revenue divided by total marketing spend, the number that cannot be double counted.
  • New-customer CAC: what it costs to win a first-time buyer, compared with first-order profit.
  • Break-even ROAS: the lowest ROAS each campaign can run at without losing money per order.
  • Contribution margin after ad spend: what is left after product cost, shipping, fees, returns and ads.
  • Average order value and repeat purchase rate: the two levers that let you afford a higher CAC.
  • Conversion rate by device: most Shopify traffic from ads is mobile, so mobile pages are checked first.

How to choose a Shopify marketing agency

Ask these questions before you sign with anyone, including us. The answers show whether an agency manages for profit or for activity.

  • Do you report from Shopify revenue and MER, or only from Ads Manager?
  • How do you set a target ROAS, and what is our break-even ROAS?
  • Who makes the ad creative, and how many new ads do you test each month?
  • What happens to email and the product pages, or do we need another agency for those?
  • Can we see real results from Shopify brands like ours, with screenshots?
  • What is the notice period, and who owns the ad accounts and creative?

Frequently asked questions

What are the best Shopify marketing agencies?

The best Shopify marketing agencies show verified results from real ad accounts, make their own ad creative, improve product pages and checkout, and report on MER and contribution margin, not just ROAS. Brand Scaling publishes its case studies, such as $146,184 in 27 days at 3.64x ROAS for a skincare brand, so you can check results before you hire.

How much does a Shopify marketing agency cost?

Expect a monthly retainer plus ad spend. Our fee is set after a Brand Growth Audit, and our partners usually spend $10,000 to $20,000 a month or more on ads.

Is it worth hiring a Shopify marketing agency?

It is worth it once you have a product people already buy and want to scale beyond what you can run yourself. Below about $20K a month in revenue, a focused freelancer may be enough.

Do you build Shopify stores?

We improve product pages, landing pages and offers for conversion, but we are not a theme development agency.

Which ad channels do you run for Shopify stores?

Meta, TikTok and Google Shopping, plus Klaviyo email and SMS for retention.

Do you work with Shopify stores across the USA?

Yes. We work with Shopify brands across the US, UK and other markets fully remotely.
