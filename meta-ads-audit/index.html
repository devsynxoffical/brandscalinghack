What does a Meta ads audit include?

A Meta ads audit reviews account structure, tracking and Conversions API quality, creative fatigue and diversity, audience overlap, bidding, landing pages and unit economics. It shows where spend is being wasted or capped, then sets priorities. Ours is the Brand Growth Audit, a one-on-one review with founder Gaurav Kapoor that ends with a 90-day plan.

What the audit reviews

Every account is different, but the same seven areas decide whether Meta can scale profitably. We look at each and rank what is costing you the most.

Account structure

How many campaigns and ad sets are splitting the budget, whether they exit the learning phase, and whether Advantage+ shopping campaigns are used where they fit. Fragmented structures are one of the most common reasons spend stalls.

Tracking, Conversions API and event match quality

Whether the pixel and Conversions API are both sending purchase events, whether they are deduplicated, how event match quality scores look, and whether Meta-reported purchases line up with Shopify orders. If tracking is off, every other decision is made on bad data.

Creative fatigue and diversity

How long winners have been running, how frequency and click-through are trending, and how many genuinely different angles are live. We map your ads against our hook and angle matrix to find what is missing.

Audience overlap

Whether prospecting, retargeting and lookalike or interest ad sets are bidding against each other for the same people, and whether exclusions are set correctly so existing customers are not counted as new ones.

Bidding and budgets

Whether lowest cost, cost caps or bid caps suit your margin and spend level, how budgets are being raised, and whether scaling steps are triggering cost spikes.

Landing pages

Mobile speed, product page clarity, offer and bundle structure, and whether the page matches the promise in the ad. Paid traffic cannot convert better than the page it lands on.

Unit economics and break-even ROAS

Gross margin, shipping, fees and AOV, turned into a break-even ROAS and break-even CAC. This sets the real target for the account, instead of a ROAS number copied from someone else.

What you receive

The output is a 90-day plan built around the four phases we use with partner brands, so you can see what happens in what order and why.

  • Foundation, days 1 to 14: tracking and CAPI fixes, offer and margin changes, product page fixes and the first creative brief.
  • Test, days 15 to 45: weekly creative batches built on the hook and angle matrix.
  • Optimize, days 45 to 90: conversion rate, AOV and ROAS work so the account can afford more spend.
  • Scale, day 90 onward: budget steps of 20% to 30%, cost caps on proven ads, and new markets or TikTok where they fit.
  • A ranked list of the issues found, with the expected effect of each fix described plainly.
  • Your break-even ROAS and CAC, so you can judge any agency or in-house buyer against the right number.

Who the audit is for

The audit is built for DTC brands with product-market fit doing roughly $20K to $2M+ a month, usually spending at least $10K to $20K a month on paid ads. That is the range where structure, tracking and creative problems cost real money and where fixing them changes the trajectory of the business.

It is a good fit if ROAS has dropped and you cannot tell why, if spend will not go up without CPA rising sharply, if Meta and Shopify numbers disagree, or if you are deciding between an agency and an in-house media buyer and want an independent view of the account first. It is a poor fit for pre-launch brands or stores without consistent sales, because there is not yet enough data to audit.

What audits usually find

Most accounts we review have two or three problems doing most of the damage, and they are rarely the ones the founder expected. The most frequent are a Conversions API setup that sends events without enough customer data to match well, a structure with too many ad sets splitting a modest budget, and a creative library where every live ad shares the same angle.

Behind those sit commercial issues: a target ROAS set without reference to margin, an AOV too low to support the CAC the market now charges, or a product page that loses mobile visitors before they see the offer. The audit separates account problems from business problems, because no amount of media buying fixes a margin that cannot pay for a customer.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Meta ads audit cost?

Prices for Meta ads audits vary widely across agencies, from free sales calls to paid deep reviews. Brand Scaling does not publish a price. The Brand Growth Audit starts with a call to confirm fit, and the scope is set after we see the account. Any ongoing management is quoted only after the review.

How long does a Meta ads audit take?

Access and data gathering usually take a few days, depending on how quickly view access to the ad account and Shopify is granted. The review itself is followed by a one-on-one walkthrough of findings and the 90-day plan. Accounts with several markets or channels take longer because each one needs its own read.

What access do you need for a Meta ads audit?

View or analyst access to your Meta Business Manager and ad account, Shopify analytics, and Events Manager so we can check the pixel, Conversions API and event match quality. If you use a separate attribution or reporting tool, view access there helps. We do not need edit access to complete the audit.

How do I audit my own Facebook ads account?

Start with tracking: compare Meta purchases with Shopify orders and check event match quality in Events Manager. Then count campaigns and ad sets, check frequency and click-through trends on your top ads, look for overlapping audiences and calculate your break-even ROAS from real margins. Fix tracking before changing anything else.

What is event match quality and why does it matter?

Event match quality is Meta's score for how well the customer information sent with an event, such as email or phone, matches Meta accounts. Higher match quality helps Meta attribute purchases and optimise delivery. Low scores usually mean the Conversions API is missing parameters, which weakens both reporting and campaign learning.

Do I have to hire you after the audit?

No. The 90-day plan is written so your team or another agency can run it. Many brands do hire us afterwards because the audit shows exactly what the work involves, but the audit is meant to be useful on its own, including a clear break-even ROAS to judge any future media buyer against.
