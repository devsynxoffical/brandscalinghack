TikTok Shop Affiliate Commission: How to Set Your Rate From Your Margins

Direct Answer: There is no single right TikTok Shop affiliate commission rate. Set it from your unit economics: take your price, subtract product cost, shipping and the TikTok referral fee, and divide what is left by the price. That percentage minus the margin you want to keep is the most commission you can afford. Run a lower open plan rate for any creator, a higher targeted rate for proven creators, and a separate Shop Ads rate for affiliate videos TikTok uses in ads.

TikTok Shop affiliate commission: The percentage of an order's value that a seller pays a creator who sells the product through a TikTok video, LIVE or showcase. The seller sets the rate in Seller Center, TikTok tracks the sale, and the commission comes out of the seller's order settlement.

What commission should you offer TikTok Shop affiliates?

Offer the highest rate your margins can carry after the TikTok referral fee and the profit you need to keep, and no higher. That number is different for every product, which is why published "typical rates" disagree so much.

As of November 2026, TikTok's Seller Center help says sellers can set an affiliate commission between 1% and 80% of each order. Third-party guides disagree on what is normal. Hamster Garage, a TikTok Shop agency, reports a US average of about 13% and a typical range of 5% to 30%, with top targeted deals reaching 50%. Other guides put open rates at 10% to 15% and targeted rates at 15% to 25%. None of these figures come from TikTok and they do not agree, so treat them as context, not a target. The useful question is what your product can afford.

How TikTok Shop affiliate commission is calculated and paid

Commission is a percentage of what the buyer actually paid for the product, minus refunds. TikTok Shop's US Seller University gives the formula as commission equals revenue minus refunds, multiplied by the commission rate. TikTok's settlement guidance describes the base as the product price excluding tax and minus seller discounts, so shipping and tax are not part of it.

  • Cancelled and refunded orders: according to TikTok, a cancelled or fully refunded order pays no commission, and a partial refund reduces it.
  • When it is paid: TikTok's US help pages say commission is finalized after delivery and the after-sales period, and is typically released around 15 days after delivery. TikTok notes it can take up to 31 days when settlement, disputes or returns cause a delay.
  • Who pays: it comes out of your order settlement.
  • Rate changes: TikTok says creators already promoting a product keep their rate for 30 days after you cut it, while an increase applies straight away. Plan cuts a month ahead.

How commission stacks with the referral fee

The referral fee is TikTok's own platform fee and the affiliate commission is what you pay the creator. They are separate, both are percentages of the sale, and both come out of the same order. So a creator order costs you both, on top of product cost and shipping. TikTok's US fee table listed a 6% referral fee for most categories from April 2024, and some 2026 seller guides report a rise to 8% that we could not confirm on an official page. Use the rate Seller Center shows for your category.

Open plan vs targeted plan

An open plan (TikTok now calls it an open collaboration) is a public rate any eligible creator can pick up. A targeted plan (target collaboration) is a private offer you send to specific creators, usually at a higher rate. TikTok's help center says that when a creator qualifies for both on the same product, the target collaboration rate takes priority, which is why TikTok advises setting it higher.

A sensible starting split

Set the open rate at a level you are happy to pay on every creator sale and keep headroom above it. If your maximum affordable rate is 30%, an open rate of 15% leaves room to offer 20% to 25% to creators who prove they can sell.

Shop Ads commission for affiliate videos in ads

TikTok lets you set a separate, usually lower, commission for orders that come from ads using a creator's video. TikTok's help center describes it as a Shop Ads commission rate you switch on inside an open collaboration. If you do not set one, the standard rate applies to ad orders too. Orders from ads that do not use affiliate posts pay no affiliate commission.

This matters because GMV Max automatically uses authorized affiliate videos. Without a Shop Ads rate, you pay full commission on sales you also paid ad spend to win.

TikTok's help center has said the Shop Ads rate in an open collaboration cannot go below 30% of the standard rate (so a 10% standard rate means at least 3%), in all regions except Mexico and Brazil. That article is more than a year old and some third-party guides quote a one-third minimum, so check the current floor in Seller Center.

How to set your rate from unit economics

Use one formula: maximum commission % = (profit before ads and commission / price) minus your target margin. Profit before ads and commission is the price minus product cost, shipping and the referral fee. Target margin is the share of the price you want to keep before any ad spend.

Worked example, using the defaults in our TikTok Shop break-even ROAS calculator: a $35 price, $8 product cost, $6 shipping and an 8% example referral fee. The referral fee is $2.80, so profit before ads and commission is $35 minus $8, $6 and $2.80, which is $18.20, or 52% of the price.

If you want to keep at least 20% of the price as profit before ads, the most you can offer is 52% minus 20%, which is 32%. If you want to keep 25%, the ceiling is 27%. For simplicity the example assumes commission is paid on the full $35 with no discounts.

Sample and seeding costs

Samples are part of your commission budget, not a separate marketing line. TikTok's Seller University says that for free samples the seller pays for the product, shipping and tax, and creators are expected to post within 14 days of receiving it. TikTok also offers refundable samples, where the creator buys the product and is refunded once their content drives a set number of sales.

In the example above, one free sample costs about $14 in product and shipping, roughly the profit from one creator order at a 15% rate. Illustrative maths: 20 samples cost $280, and the few creators whose videos sell have to cover all of it.

  • Approve free samples for creators whose past sales and content fit your product, not every request.
  • Use refundable samples for unproven creators so the sample pays for itself only when it sells.

Tiering commission by creator performance

Tie higher rates to results, not follower counts. Tiers keep costs predictable and give creators a reason to keep posting.

  • Base tier: your open collaboration rate, available to everyone, set well below your maximum.
  • Proven tier: a targeted rate a few points higher for creators who have driven sales of your product, reviewed every month.
  • Top tier: your highest targeted rate, close to your ceiling, for the small group that drives most of your affiliate revenue.
  • Ad tier: a Shop Ads rate below the standard rate, so videos that GMV Max scales do not carry full commission on top of ad spend.

Avoid creeping toward the ceiling

Every tier must sit under your maximum affordable rate. If top creators want more, offer early access, exclusive bundles or flat fees for specific videos instead.

When to raise your commission rate

Raise your rate when the issue is creator interest and your margins have room, not when the product itself is not selling. A higher rate can attract creators but cannot fix a weak listing, price or content.

  • Raise it when few creators request samples or add the product, and your rate is well below your ceiling.
  • Raise it for a launch, knowing a later cut takes 30 days to apply.
  • Do not raise it if creators are posting but videos do not convert. Fix the offer, the listing or the creator brief first.
  • Do not raise it if ads are already running on affiliate videos at the standard rate. Set a Shop Ads rate first.

Honest caveats

No rate guarantees creator interest. TikTok's rules and fees change often and differ by country, so check Seller Center before each change. Our TikTok Shop agency team can set and run your affiliate plans, starting with a Brand Growth Audit.
