Meta Ads Library (Facebook Ads Library): A free transparency tool at facebook.com/ads/library that lists every ad currently running on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Audience Network, with the start date, platforms, creative versions and advertiser page. It does not show spend, reach or results for normal commercial ads outside the EU, and you do not need an ad account or a paid spy tool to use it.

What is the Meta Ads Library?

The Meta Ads Library (many people still call it the Facebook Ads Library) is a free tool from Meta that shows every ad currently running on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Audience Network. You can search by keyword or brand name, filter by country, and see the date each ad started running. You do not need an ad account, a Business Manager or even a Facebook login to use it.

For each ad you see the advertiser's page, the start date, the platforms it runs on and every creative version under the same ad. What you do not see for normal ecommerce ads in the US, UK, Canada or Australia is spend, impressions, CTR or ROAS. That is why media buyers read how long an ad has run, and how many versions a brand keeps live, as the signal for what is making money.

For a DTC brand it is the cheapest market research you can do: it shows you exactly which ads your biggest competitors keep paying for. The official tool lives at facebook.com/ads/library.

How to find winning ads in the Meta Ads Library

This is the exact process from the video, run for a DTC skincare client selling a vitamin C serum.

1. Search your product, not just your competitors

Open the Meta Ads Library, set the country to where your customers are (for our client, the United States), choose All ads and search for your product category. For this client we searched "vitamin C serum". This shows every brand advertising that product, including ones you did not know about.

If the category is stuck on Issues, elections or politics you will see no ecommerce ads at all. Switching it to All ads is the most common fix when the library looks empty.

2. Keep only ads that have run for 2 weeks or more

Every ad shows a Started running on date. Two weeks is enough to prove an ad, whether the brand spends $20 or $100 a day on it. If a big brand has kept an ad live for 2 to 3 months, it is profitable. An ad that started 12 days ago is not proven yet, so skip it.

Also check how many ads use the same creative. When a brand runs the same creative in 3 or more ads, it is scaling a winner.

3. Check the brand's own Facebook page too

One search does not always show everything. Open the brand's Facebook page and its Ad library link to see all of its active ads in one place, then open See ad details to count the versions of each concept and click through to the landing page behind it.

Why those ads work: 5 patterns we copied

Finding a winner is half the job. The other half is understanding why it wins, so you copy the structure, not the ad.

The message lands in 5 to 8 seconds

One winning vitamin C serum ad made its whole point in 6 seconds. Your hook has 5 to 8 seconds to stop the scroll and state the benefit. If you need to explain more, keep the full ad under 30 to 40 seconds.

Trust comes first in skincare

The same ad opened with "recommended by US dermatologists". People are skeptical about putting a product they bought online on their skin. Proof (dermatologist recommended, clinically tested, verified reviews) answers that objection before it forms.

Results without "before and after"

One winning ad showed hands at day 1 and day 30 without ever saying "before and after". Meta restricts before and after claims for personal health and beauty products, and ads that use them often get flagged. Show the change; do not label it.

Amazon reviews inside the creative

Many DTC brands also sell on Amazon. One brand put a screenshot of a real Amazon review straight into the ad. Viewers who check Amazon find the same reviews, which removes the last doubt.

Sell the routine, not the single product

A carousel retargeting ad showed a full skincare routine: cleanser, vitamin C serum, retinol, soothing cream. Selling a routine lifts order value and works well with subscriptions. On our client's landing page we added "clinically recommended: 3 months for best results", so buyers choose the 3 month plan and stay long enough to see results.

Our DTC Meta ads testing playbook

Research only pays off when it turns into tests. We test at $20 per ad set. You can go down to $10 or up to $50 depending on your budget; this example assumes a brand that can spend $150 to $200 a day on testing.

  • Objective: conversions, optimised for purchase.
  • $20 per ad set, 7 to 10 ad sets, 1 to 2 creatives per ad set.
  • Run for 3 days. This is the validation phase.
  • Checkpoints over 14 days: day 1, 4, 5, 7, 10 and 14.

How we find proven buyers from comments

Find a competitor ad with thousands of shares and positive comments. Open the profiles of people who say "I have used this brand for years" and look at the pages they like. Add those brands as interests in Ads Manager. These are people who already buy in your category.

Audience size rules

  • Keep each ad set close to, but under, 5 million people.
  • Combine 2 to 3 interests from one group with 2 to 3 from another, then narrow with your main keyword to hit that size.
  • Do not use the same keyword in more than 3 ad sets.

KPIs, test setup and when to kill an ad set

Track the whole funnel, not just ROAS, so you can see where a losing ad set breaks.

The KPIs we track

  • Delivery: link clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, landing page views, amount spent, frequency.
  • Funnel: view content, add to cart and cost per add to cart, initiate checkout, cost per purchase.
  • Revenue: purchase conversion value, ROAS, average order value.
  • Video: 25%, 50%, 75% and 95% watch rates.

When to kill an ad set

  • CTR under 1% after a full day and $20 spent: people are not responding to the offer.
  • CPC above $1 with no conversions.
  • CPA at 2 to 3 times your target with no improvement.
  • ROAS under your minimum (for this client, 2).
  • Clicks but no view content events: people are not reaching the landing page.
  • No meaningful action by day 3.

Scaling winners into a CBO campaign

Over the 14 day test you kill losers and keep 2 to 3 performers. Ad sets with CTR above 1.5% and a profitable CPA or positive ROAS move into one CBO (campaign budget optimization) campaign.

  • Put 3 to 5 proven ad sets in the CBO.
  • Set the CBO budget at 3 to 5 times your target CPA. Example: target CPA $20, CBO budget about $100 a day.
  • Keep scaling while the CBO stays profitable.

When to pause or adjust the CBO

  • CPA rises above 2 times your target.
  • CTR drops by 1 point: a sign of creative fatigue.
  • ROAS falls below your minimum.
  • Conversions drop on creatives that used to work: launch new iterations of the winning angle.

Searching the library like a media buyer

Keyword searches that work for ecommerce. Search for offer language, not just product names: free shipping, bundle, limited drop, buy 2 get 1, 30 day guarantee. Combine with one niche word, for example bundle skincare or free shipping serum. Offer words surface ads that are built to convert.

Meta ad library search by ID. Every ad has a numeric Library ID shown on its card and in its URL. Set the search type to Ad ID and paste it to see that ad with all of its versions. Use this when a teammate or creator sends you a link.

Instagram only. Set the platform filter to Instagram. Vertical 9:16 Reels placements usually mean the brand has a creator led UGC pipeline, which is the fastest production model to copy.

Can you sort by impressions? Not for commercial ads. Sorting by impressions and spend ranges are only available for ads about social issues, elections or politics. Run time plus version count is the closest free proxy.

Downloads, the API and when the library is not working

Downloading videos. There is no download button. For internal research teams screen record the ad or save it with a browser tool. Keep it in a private swipe file and recreate the structure with your own product and footage; never re-upload a competitor's ad, footage or creators.

The Meta Ad Library API. It is free but limited: it covers ads about social issues, elections and politics plus EU transparency data, and needs a Meta developer account with confirmed identity. For ecommerce competitor research the web tool shows the same commercial ad fields, so most brands do not need the API.

When the library is not working, check these in order: the country is set to your market; the category is All ads; ad blockers and privacy extensions are off (try a private window); the search type matches what you pasted (Keyword vs Ad ID); and if nothing else works, Meta's tool may be down for a short time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Meta Ads Library free?

Yes. The Meta Ads Library is free and public. You can search all active Facebook and Instagram ads without logging in or having an ad account.

How long should an ad run before it counts as a winner?

At least 2 weeks. Brands do not keep paying for ads that lose money, so an ad running for 2 weeks or more, especially one used in several ads, is a strong winner signal.

How much should I spend to test Meta ads?

We test at $20 per ad set across 7 to 10 ad sets for 3 days. You can start at $10 per ad set on a smaller budget, but you need enough spend for Meta to show results.

When should I kill a Meta ad set?

Kill it if CTR is under 1% after a day, CPC is above $1 with no sales, or CPA stays at 2 to 3 times your target. If nothing meaningful happens by day 3, turn it off.

Can I use before and after images in skincare ads?

Meta restricts before and after claims for personal health and beauty products, and these ads often get rejected. Show day 1 and day 30 visually instead of labelling it before and after.

Can you see ad spend in the Meta Ads Library?

Not for normal commercial ads in the US, UK, Canada or Australia. Spend ranges and impressions are shown only for ads about social issues, elections or politics, and ads delivered in the EU show reach data.

Can I search the Meta Ads Library by ad ID?

Yes. Set the search type to Ad ID and paste the numeric Library ID from the ad card or its URL to see that ad with all of its versions.

What is a CBO campaign in Meta ads?

CBO (campaign budget optimization, now called Advantage campaign budget) sets one budget at campaign level and lets Meta move it to the best ad sets. We move 3 to 5 proven ad sets into a CBO with a budget of 3 to 5 times target CPA.
