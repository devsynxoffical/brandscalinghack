Ecommerce advertising is not one channel. Meta and TikTok put your product in front of people who were not looking for it; Google Shopping and search catch people who already are. Most growing brands need both, in the right order, with budgets that respect their margins.

We run ecommerce advertising as one plan across Meta, TikTok and Google, so the channels do not double count each other and every dollar is judged against your break-even ROAS and your Shopify revenue.

What is ecommerce advertising?

Ecommerce advertising is paid promotion that sends shoppers to an online store: social ads on Meta and TikTok that create demand, and search and Shopping ads on Google that capture people already looking. An ecommerce ads agency plans the channel mix, makes the creative, runs the campaigns and judges them on profit per order, not on clicks or reach.

The ecommerce advertising channels we run

Each channel does a different job. These are the ones that matter for most DTC brands, and what we use each for.

Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram)

The main source of new customers for most DTC brands. Broad and Advantage+ campaigns, fed by a steady supply of new creative, find buyers the brand could not target by hand.

TikTok ads and Spark Ads

Short creator-style video reaches younger audiences at a different cost. Spark Ads let us pay to boost the best organic and creator videos.

Google Shopping

Product listings with price and image for people already searching. Feed quality, product titles and margin-based bidding decide how well they work.

Performance Max

Google's automated campaign across Search, Shopping, YouTube and Display. We split brand and non-brand so it does not take credit for sales you would get anyway.

Google Search ads

Text ads for high-intent searches, including your brand name and competitor terms, where a buyer is close to purchase.

Retargeting

Catalog and video retargeting for visitors who did not buy, kept small and frequency-capped so it does not take credit for sales that would have happened anyway.

Ecommerce PPC: where Google fits

Ecommerce PPC usually means Google Shopping, Performance Max and search ads, where you pay per click. It works best for products people already look for, and it depends on a clean product feed: accurate titles, prices, images, GTINs and stock.

We set Google bids from your margin per product, keep brand and non-brand spending apart, and compare Google-reported sales with Shopify so the account is judged on real orders. For brands whose demand comes mostly from social, PPC stays smaller and Meta does the heavy lifting.

How much should an ecommerce brand spend on ads?

Work backwards from your margin and goal, not from a percentage someone else uses. Start with your break-even ROAS, the return at which an order stops losing money after product cost, shipping, fees and returns. Then set the monthly budget your revenue goal needs at a ROAS comfortably above that line.

The free Meta ads budget calculator turns a revenue goal into daily spend, target CPA and weekly purchases, and the break-even ROAS calculator shows the floor every campaign must stay above. Partner brands typically spend at least $10,000 to $20,000 a month on ads.

How we judge ecommerce ads

Each platform reports its own conversions, and they overlap. We manage campaigns on platform data and judge the business on numbers that cannot be double counted.

  • MER: total store revenue divided by total ad spend across every channel.
  • New-customer CAC compared with first-order profit and LTV.
  • Break-even ROAS per campaign, from your real margins.
  • Creative hit rate: how many new ads beat the current winners each month.
  • Shopify orders versus platform-reported purchases, to catch tracking gaps.
  • Contribution margin after ad spend, week by week.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an ecommerce ads agency cost?

Ecommerce ads agencies charge a monthly retainer, a percentage of ad spend, a performance fee or a mix. Brand Scaling quotes after the free Brand Growth Audit, because the scope depends on your channels, spend and creative volume. Partner brands typically spend at least $10,000 to $20,000 a month on ads.

What is an ecommerce PPC agency?

An ecommerce PPC agency runs pay-per-click advertising for online stores, mainly Google Shopping, Performance Max and search ads, and often Microsoft Ads. Many also run Meta and TikTok. We run all of them together so budgets move to whichever channel brings customers at the best cost.

Should an ecommerce brand advertise on Meta or Google first?

If people do not yet search for your product, start with Meta to create demand. If they already search for it, Google Shopping captures that intent at a lower cost per sale. Many brands end up running both, with budgets judged on total MER rather than each platform's own numbers.

What is a good ROAS for ecommerce ads?

Any ROAS comfortably above your break-even ROAS, which is 1 divided by your contribution margin. Across our 25 published case studies the median is 3.97x, but a 60%-margin brand can be profitable at 2x and a 25%-margin brand needs more than 4x.

How long until ecommerce advertising works?

Tracking and offer fixes happen in the first 7 to 14 days. Most brands see measurable change in CPA, AOV and ROAS within 2 to 4 weeks of the first creative wave, and a stable scaling budget is the goal by day 90.
