Frequently Asked Questions

What is DTC marketing?

DTC marketing is how brands that sell directly to customers, usually through their own Shopify store, find and keep buyers without retailers in between. It combines paid social and search ads, creative, landing pages, conversion rate work and email or SMS, and it is measured on revenue, new-customer CAC and contribution margin.

How do you scale DTC brands with retargeting?

Keep retargeting small and let prospecting do the scaling. We run catalog retargeting for recent visitors and cart abandoners at about 10% to 15% of spend, exclude recent buyers, change the message to the offer and deadline, and judge it on blended MER, because retargeting takes credit for many sales that would happen anyway.

What does a DTC marketing agency do?

It plans and runs the paid ads, creative, landing pages and retention that grow a direct-to-consumer brand. A good DTC agency reports on profit metrics like MER and new-customer CAC, not just ROAS.

How much does a DTC marketing agency cost?

Most charge a monthly retainer, sometimes plus a share of ad spend or revenue. Our fee is quoted after a Brand Growth Audit, and partners typically spend at least $10,000 to $20,000 a month on ads.

What is the difference between a DTC agency and an ecommerce growth agency?

They overlap. A DTC agency focuses on brands selling direct to customers. An ecommerce growth agency also covers marketplaces and TikTok Shop. We do both.

Which brands do you work with?

DTC and ecommerce brands doing about $20K to $2M+ a month with proven demand, in apparel, beauty, skincare, supplements, home and more.

How fast will we see results?

The first 14 days fix tracking, offers and landing pages. New creative tests start in the first month, and scaling begins once winners hold their CAC.

Do you work with brands outside the US?

Yes. Published results include a UK skincare brand that did €118,000 in its first month.
